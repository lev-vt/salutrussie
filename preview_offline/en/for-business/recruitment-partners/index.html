For overseas recruitment agencies

A Russian legal partner for international worker placement projects.

Connect recruitment with the Russian legal route

If your agency sources candidates for a Russian employer, the immigration process should be mapped before a start date is promised. SalutRussie can act as Russian legal counsel: review the structure, document the route and support the agreed immigration filings. Candidate sourcing and government decisions remain with the relevant parties.

Recruitment agency

Candidate sourcing, skills checks, communication and compliance with rules in the country of origin.

Russian employer

Vacancies, employment terms, contracts, worksite and the employer’s obligations in Russia.

SalutRussie

Russian legal assessment, document map, support with agreed applications and immigration risk review.

Start with a short project brief

  1. Share the agency profile and registration details without candidate personal data.
  2. Describe the employer, roles, worksite, headcount and intended dates.
  3. We identify the legal questions and agree who handles each task.
  4. Once scope is agreed, we prepare a document and decision map.

For agencies in India

Check your status and the applicable Indian overseas recruitment rules independently. The Indian Ministry of External Affairs guidance on Recruiting Agents is a useful starting point. Indian registration does not replace Russian immigration permissions or notifications; the Russian route needs a separate review.

Frequently asked questions

Can we promise candidates a departure date?

A date should not be treated as guaranteed before the route, documents and authority decisions are checked.

Will you recruit candidates?

This page covers Russian legal work. Recruitment and candidate communication are allocated separately in the project scope.

Russian starting points: Federal Law No. 115-FZ and Ministry Order No. 290 of 12 May 2026. The route depends on status and contract structure.
