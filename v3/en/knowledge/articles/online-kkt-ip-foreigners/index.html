Online cash registers and payment receipts for an IP

Editorial edition · 24 November 2026

SalutRussie editorial teamFounder in legal practice since 2005Editorial version: 24 November 2026

In brief — Cash-register duties depend on the seller, customer, transaction and payment method — not on citizenship or whether the payment reaches a bank account. Online payment does not automatically remove the receipt obligation.

Classify transactions

Payments by consumers, including online card and other electronic payments, generally require a fiscal receipt when Law 54-FZ applies. Non-cash payments between organizations and individual entrepreneurs without presentation of an electronic payment instrument may fall within a statutory exemption. Examine the actual settlement method and any special exceptions.

Arrange the process

If required, register an eligible cash register, configure a fiscal data operator and ensure the receipt is issued with the correct particulars. Agree who generates the receipt when using a payment provider or agent. An ordinary bank transfer confirmation is not necessarily a fiscal receipt.

Before launch

Map payments, advances, refunds, partial payments and final settlements. Test receipt generation and correction procedures. Check the applicable tax regime and sector exemptions with the Federal Tax Service before accepting payments.

Legal basis and official sources

Federal Law No. 54-FZ on cash register equipment. Official publication portal · Federal Tax Service.
