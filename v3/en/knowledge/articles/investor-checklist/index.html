Legal due diligence before investing in Russia

Editorial edition · 24 November 2026

In brief — A persuasive presentation is not proof of ownership, profitability or the investor's rights. Establish the counterparty, the asset, existing liabilities and your exit mechanism before signing or transferring money.

Verify the legal facts

Obtain current company registry information, constituent documents and signatory authority. Establish the legal owner of the asset and restrictions, pledges, litigation and required approvals. For real estate, verify EGRN records through official channels. Cross-check all evidence against the transaction documents.

Design the deal

Specify the asset or interest acquired, payment milestones, conditions precedent, representations, reporting and decision rights. Agree the consequences of breach and a workable exit and dispute mechanism. Assess any applicable cross-border or sectoral restrictions.

Record the outcome

Separate verified facts, outstanding information and conditions to closing. Have financial, accounting and tax assumptions checked by appropriate specialists. Legal due diligence reduces uncertainty but cannot guarantee a return.

Legal basis and official sources

Civil Code; Federal Tax Service and EGRN registers. Official publication portal · Federal Tax Service.
