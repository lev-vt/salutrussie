Can a foreign national register as an individual entrepreneur?

Editorial edition · 24 November 2026

SalutRussie editorial teamFounder in legal practice since 2005Editorial version: 24 November 2026

In brief — Foreign citizens may register as individual entrepreneurs in Russia if the statutory requirements are met, including documentation of the right to temporary or permanent residence. A visitor visa or migration card alone is not equivalent to the required residence document.

Prepare the registration

Check your RVP or VNZh, registered address and identity documents. Confirm translation and certification requirements. Use the current R21001 form and official Federal Tax Service procedure. Check grounds for refusal and restrictions for your intended activity.

After registration

Select an eligible tax regime, identify reporting and contribution obligations, and assess cash-register, banking and licence requirements. The registration itself does not resolve every immigration or employment question.

Maintain your residence status

Expiry or cancellation of the document confirming the right to reside can affect the IP registration under Law 129-FZ. Review status before accepting long-term contracts. Consider an LLC separately if IP is not suitable.

Legal basis and official sources

Article 22.1, Federal Law No. 129-FZ. Official publication portal · Federal Tax Service.
