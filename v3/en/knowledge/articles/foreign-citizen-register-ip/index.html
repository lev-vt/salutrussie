How to register as a sole trader in Russia as a foreign national: residence permits, documents and registration

Sole trader registration in Russia for foreign nationals: residence status, registered address, form R21001, state duty, deadlines, refusals by the tax authority and choice of tax regime.

Lev VtiurinLawyer and founder of SalutRussie · In legal practice since 2005SalutRussie editorial teamLast reviewed: 24 November 2026

In brief. A foreign national can register as an individual entrepreneur, or sole trader (IP), in Russia if they hold a valid temporary residence permit (RVP), temporary residence permit for educational purposes (RVPO) or permanent residence permit (VNZh) and meet the other legal requirements. An ordinary visa or migration card alone does not confer this right. This article explains the documents, registration procedure, deadlines and steps to take after starting your business.

Which foreign nationals can register as sole traders?

A foreign national may register as an individual entrepreneur if they hold a document confirming their right to reside temporarily or permanently in Russia and meet the other statutory requirements.

Document or statusCan you register as a sole trader?
VNZhYes, subject to the other requirements.
RVP, including permits under Decree 702Yes, subject to the other requirements.
RVPO — temporary residence permit for educational purposesYes. The Federal Tax Service expressly recognises the RVPO as a qualifying document.
Only a tourist, business, work or student visaNo. A visa alone does not establish residence status.

For certain special statuses, including recognised refugee status or temporary asylum, the eligibility of the relevant documents must be assessed separately. In addition to immigration requirements, general restrictions on sole trader registration and restrictions on particular business activities apply.

What address do you need for registration?

As a general rule, an individual entrepreneur is registered at their place of residence. If the identity document and the document confirming residence rights do not contain an address, the applicant must provide the prescribed evidence of their address (Article 22.1 of Federal Law No. 129-FZ).

Registration at your place of residence and migration registration at your place of stay are different procedures. The FNS permits applications at the place of stay where there is no registered place of residence. For foreign nationals, however, it is necessary to establish separately whether residence rights and the address are documented in accordance with the requirements for sole trader registration. A tenancy agreement or the detachable confirmation section of an arrival notification alone does not guarantee acceptance.

Do not buy fictitious registration. If the FNS does not accept your address, establish the precise grounds for refusal first. More about migration registration in rented accommodation →

Foreign-language documents must be accompanied by a duly prepared Russian translation. Notarisation of copies depends on the filing method: the law provides exceptions for personal applications directly to the registering authority with original documents and for properly authenticated electronic submissions.

If you have already been assigned a taxpayer identification number (INN), include it. Not having a previously assigned INN is not in itself a ground for refusal: the tax authority will register the entrepreneur for tax purposes under the prescribed procedure.

How do you apply, how much does it cost and how long does it take?

Submit the documents to the authorised FNS registration office. You may apply in person, through a multifunctional public service centre (MFC), through a notary, by post or electronically, subject to signature and document authentication requirements.

ParameterAs at 24 November 2026
State registrationNo more than three working days from submission of documents to the registering authority.
State duty for ordinary paper filingRUB 800.
Electronic submissionNo state duty is charged, including electronic submission through an MFC or notary. Notarial services are paid for separately.
ResultAn electronic EGRIP registration record; paper confirmation is available upon request under the prescribed procedure.

Check the EGRIP information after registration. Preparing translations, arranging a notarial appointment or transmitting documents through an intermediary may increase the overall time required.

Choice of business activity and immigration restrictions

Choose your primary and additional OKVED codes. Check whether a licence, permit or compliance with special conditions is required. Listing an activity code does not itself authorise a regulated activity.

If you hold an RVP or RVPO, assess the regional and other restrictions of your status separately, especially if you plan to work personally or conduct ongoing business activity in another region. The place of sole trader registration, your customers' locations and the place where activities are actually carried out are not necessarily the same.

What to do after registration and before receiving your first payment

  1. Choose your tax regime. A newly registered sole trader can notify the tax authority of a transition to the simplified tax system (USN) upon registration or within 30 calendar days of tax registration. Check the separate criteria and deadlines for the automated simplified tax system (AUSN), the patent tax system and other regimes.
  2. Arrange business payments and open a business bank account if necessary. Banks verify documents and transactions under their own lawful procedures.
  3. Check whether you need an online cash register before accepting payments from customers.
  4. Set up accounting, mandatory payments and reporting. Under many regimes, insurance contributions are due even when there is no revenue, unless an exemption applies.
  5. If you work with overseas customers, assess currency and tax regulations, contracts and payments separately.

What happens to your sole trader registration if your residence permit is replaced or lost?

Important rule: a foreign national's sole trader registration ceases to be valid on the date their residence document is cancelled or expires (Article 22.3(11) of Federal Law No. 129-FZ). Do not assume that an old EGRIP entry preserves the right to trade after that date.

When a document is replaced, or its type, details or validity period change, notify the registering authority within seven working days under the prescribed procedure. For example, when moving from an RVP to a VNZh, update your EGRIP details in time. To update the register, submit form R24001 and a copy of the document confirming the change. See the FNS guidance for foreign entrepreneurs (in Russian).

Registering as a sole trader does not itself confer an RVP or VNZh or exempt you from annual residence notifications and other immigration obligations. From 2027, consider the revised business activity and income documentation requirements for the relevant categories of foreign nationals.

What if the tax authority refuses your application?

Obtain the decision stating the precise ground for refusal and record the date you received it. Establish whether the refusal concerns a missing document, an error in form R21001, the address or a legal barrier.

If the refusal is due to failure to provide required documents or failure to comply with application form and formatting requirements, the law allows one additional submission after correcting the issue, without paying the state duty again, within three months of the refusal decision. This rule does not automatically apply to every refusal.

If the refusal is unlawful, a complaint may be submitted to the higher registration authority, generally within three months of receipt of the decision by the applicant. For further appeals, take into account the mandatory pre-trial complaint procedure and court filing deadlines.

Do not resubmit the same documents without assessing the grounds for refusal.

Sole trader or limited liability company: which is better for a foreign national?

Frequently asked questions

Can I register as a sole trader with an RVP obtained under Decree 702?

Yes, provided your RVP is valid and you meet the other requirements.

Do I need a Russian business partner?

No. Sole trader registration is a status held by one individual; there are no co-founders.

Can I also work under an employment contract?

Yes. Sole trader status does not itself prevent employment. You must comply with applicable immigration, employment and tax rules.

Can I register as a sole trader with only a visa and then obtain permanent residence?

An ordinary visa alone is not a qualifying basis for sole trader registration. First obtain an appropriate residence status or consider another lawful business structure.

Key takeaway

The right sequence for a foreign entrepreneur is to obtain valid residence status, confirm an address, choose business activities and taxation, register as a sole trader, and then arrange operations and payments.
