Migration registration in rented accommodation: how foreign nationals can register themselves

How to register in a rented apartment in Russia: rental agreements, self-filing with the Ministry of Internal Affairs or through Gosuslugi, documents, deadlines, state fees and Moscow rules.

Lev VtiurinLawyer and founder of SalutRussie · In legal practice since 2005SalutRussie editorial teamLast reviewed: 24 November 2026

In brief. If you rent an apartment in Russia and have concluded a residential tenancy agreement with your host, you may arrange your own migration registration in the cases provided for by law. Your landlord does not always need to attend the Ministry of Internal Affairs (MVD) in person or confirm your application through Gosuslugi. Choose the applicable procedure, prepare the necessary documents and check any special regional rules.

Can you register without your landlord attending in person?

Yes. If you have concluded a residential tenancy agreement with your host and actually live in the apartment, Article 22(3.5) of Federal Law No. 109-FZ allows you to submit your own arrival notification. You can apply in person at an MVD office or use the electronic procedure.

This does not mean you can register in any apartment without the consent of the person providing accommodation. You need a valid agreement, actual residence and the prescribed documents. However, the separate electronic confirmation of the owner's consent under Article 22(3.3) is not an independent condition for filing specifically under a tenancy agreement.

Registration at your place of stay or place of residence?

Your statusRequired procedure
Visa or visa-exempt temporary stayGenerally, migration registration at your place of stay; check any special regimes.
Temporary residence permit (RVP), temporary residence permit for educational purposes (RVPO) or permanent residence permit (VNZh), with the right to use the apartment chosen as your place of residenceRegistration at your place of residence.
RVP or VNZh holder temporarily staying at another addressRegistration at your place of stay in the cases prescribed by law.

Registration at your place of residence is a separate procedure. Apply within seven working days of receiving the relevant residence status or arriving at your chosen place of residence. Do not confuse it with submitting an arrival notification.

Deadlines and state fees

The general deadline for an arrival notification is seven working days after arriving at your place of stay. Hotels have a shorter special deadline: no later than the following working day. Exceptions apply to highly qualified specialists (HQS), their family members and nationals of certain countries, including under international agreements.

For a temporarily staying foreign national, the ordinary migration registration period cannot exceed one year and does not replace a lawful basis for staying in Russia. A one-year tenancy agreement does not itself extend your visa or permitted stay.

Other special grounds also exist: accommodation owned by the foreign national, documented circumstances preventing the host from filing, staying at the address specified in the invitation application for visa-based entry, and self-filing by a VNZh holder with the host's written consent. Each route has its own conditions.

Documents for self-filing under a tenancy agreement

Prepare an arrival notification and the following documents under the migration registration rules:

  • Copies of the pages of identity documents for the foreign national and their children containing personal details and border-crossing endorsements.
  • Copies of migration cards where required; the MVD may verify card details through interagency information exchange.
  • A copy of the tenancy agreement concluded with the host.
  • A copy of the identity document of the host or the responsible representative of an organisation.
  • When filing for children under 18 named in the agreement, evidence of the family relationship with the translation or translator's signature duly notarised.
  • Confirmation of payment of the state fee where applicable; a separate payment document is not required for electronic filing, and payment details alone are sufficient in cases specified by the rules.

Check the current notification form and documents establishing lawful stay. If a representative signs the agreement, verify their authority. Keep the stamped detachable confirmation section of the notification or the electronic document signed by the MVD in your online account.

Special rules in Moscow and the Moscow Region: the Amina app

From 1 September 2025 to 1 September 2029, a pilot scheme for special migration registration operates in Moscow and the Moscow Region. From 1 September 2026, it also covers statutory categories of visa-exempt foreign nationals temporarily staying there for non-employment purposes for more than 90 calendar days.

Participants report their actual location through the Amina app. Ordinary migration registration under a tenancy agreement generally does not apply to them. Participants must report changes in their actual location within three working days, subject to statutory exceptions. Absence of geolocation data for more than three working days is a ground for removal from registration at the authorised organisation's address. Where there is no lawful basis for staying, this may result in inclusion in the controlled persons register. Hotels and other institutions specified by law record arrivals and departures through their administration (Article 5(8), (15), (18) and (21) of Federal Law No. 121-FZ).

The pilot does not automatically apply to every foreign national in Moscow. For visa holders, RVP and VNZh holders, minors and Belarusian citizens, determine the applicable ordinary or other special procedure. The app does not extend your lawful period of stay.

What if your landlord does not want to register a foreign tenant?

Find out why the owner is unwilling. If the owner is prepared to conclude a tenancy agreement but does not want to visit the MVD, self-filing under the agreement may resolve the issue. Agree in advance on the provision of necessary document copies and information.

If the owner refuses to conclude an agreement or provide documents, do not buy registration at someone else's address. First resolve the legal basis for occupying the apartment or find alternative accommodation.

What apartment owners need to know

Migration registration does not grant a foreign national ownership rights or a share in the apartment. The right to occupy the accommodation, termination of that right and the procedure for vacating the property are governed by law and the tenancy agreement.

Owners should verify actual residence, the accuracy of information and the parties' authority, and comply with applicable host obligations. Migration registration does not replace a tenancy agreement or remove tax obligations arising from rental income.

What to do after moving or extending your permitted stay

If you move, register at your new address within the applicable deadlines. If your passport details change or your lawful stay is extended, check whether you must update the registration information. Obtaining an RVP, RVPO or VNZh may require registration at your place of residence.

If an electronic notification is not accepted, keep the application reference and error message. Do not wait until the deadline expires: check whether you can file in person at the MVD or use another permitted method.

Registration at an address where a foreign national does not intend to stay or where accommodation is not actually provided may result in serious liability, including criminal liability for fictitious migration registration.

Frequently asked questions

Does my landlord need to come to the MVD with me?

Not necessarily. When you file independently under a qualifying tenancy agreement, the law does not require the owner's personal attendance, but the required documents must be submitted.

Can I submit a notification for my child?

Yes. Under the tenancy agreement procedure, you can file for your own children under 18 named in the agreement, provided you establish the family relationship.

Is the Submitted status on Gosuslugi sufficient?

No. Check the outcome and retain the electronic detachable confirmation section signed by the MVD.

Does migration registration extend my visa?

No. Your visa, permitted stay and migration registration are separate legal matters.

Key takeaway

If you rent an apartment in Russia, start by checking the tenancy agreement and your actual address. Depending on your status, you may be able to arrange migration registration independently, without the owner visiting the MVD in person.
