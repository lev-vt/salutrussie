Migration registration of a foreign tenant

Editorial edition · 24 November 2026

In brief — Migration registration is tied to the actual place of stay, not a purchased address. The receiving party usually notifies the authorities, but the law also provides routes for a foreign tenant to act personally in specified circumstances.

Who can submit

Under Article 22(3.5), a foreign citizen who has concluded the qualifying lease with the receiving party may personally notify arrival under the statutory procedure. Attach the required lease copy and, for accompanying minor children where applicable, evidence of the relationship. Other electronic procedures have their own requirements.

Deadlines and proof

The general notification period is seven working days, subject to exceptions, treaties and special regimes. Hotels and certain institutions follow different deadlines. Check your status and region. Keep the detachable confirmation or other official evidence.

Avoid fictitious registration

Do not pay for an address where you will not actually stay. Put the landlord's cooperation and the actual address in the lease. An owner, tenant and agent should understand their distinct duties; obtain advice if the receiving party refuses.

Legal basis and official sources

Article 22, Federal Law No. 109-FZ. Official publication portal · Federal Tax Service.
