Permanent residence with a Russian honours degree

Editorial edition · 24 November 2026

In brief — A qualifying honours diploma from a full-time, state-accredited higher education programme in Russia can provide a direct route to VNZh without an earlier RVP. A diploma alone does not resolve lawful stay or every general requirement.

Verify the four conditions

Check the place of education, full-time format, state accreditation of the programme and the actual honours diploma. A master's degree may qualify if these conditions are met. Keep documentary evidence of the programme, not only its reputation.

Plan filing and lawful stay

Check the visa or education residence permit dates and the document list before graduation. Obtain formal acceptance of the application. If an authority demands additional employment evidence, distinguish an initial eligibility condition from a separate income rule; request the legal basis for the requirement.

Changes from 2027

New cancellation provisions applicable from 1 January 2027 address employment and income for certain VNZh grounds, including the honours-diploma route. The new post-issuance conditions must not be confused with an initial universal requirement to work in the field of study. Check the exact provision, timing and exceptions.

Legal basis and official sources

Article 8(2)(11) of Law No. 115-FZ; Law No. 241-FZ. Official publication portal · Federal Tax Service.
