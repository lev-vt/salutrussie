Visa expiry while an RVP or VNZh application is pending

Editorial edition · 24 November 2026

SalutRussie editorial teamFounder in legal practice since 2005Editorial version: 24 November 2026

In brief — Acceptance of certain RVP documents or a VNZh application can affect the obligation to leave Russia under Article 5(2). Do not confuse acceptance, extension of permitted stay, the visa document and migration registration.

Establish acceptance

Find the official acceptance document and filing date. Check exactly which application and accompanying documents were accepted. An appointment, incomplete package or refusal to accept papers is not the same event.

Confirm the practical procedure

Before the visa expires, request confirmation of the lawful basis for remaining and the required procedure for stay, registration and any new or extended visa. The competent authority and documents depend on status. Keep originals and written communications.

Travel and work are separate

A lawful stay pending a decision does not automatically permit international travel and re-entry on an expired visa or new employment. Do not leave without checking entry rights and the effect on the pending application. Seek urgent advice if a deadline is close.

Legal basis and official sources

Article 5(2), Federal Law No. 115-FZ. Official publication portal · Federal Tax Service.
