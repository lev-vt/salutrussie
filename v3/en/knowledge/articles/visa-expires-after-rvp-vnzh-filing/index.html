Your visa expires after applying for residence: how to stay in Russia legally

How to remain in Russia after applying for a temporary residence permit (RVP) or permanent residence permit (VNZh): visa extensions, permitted stay, migration registration and re-entry rules.

Lev VtiurinLawyer and founder of SalutRussie · In legal practice since 2005SalutRussie editorial teamLast reviewed: 24 November 2026

In brief. If the Ministry of Internal Affairs (MVD) has accepted your RVP documents or VNZh application before your permitted stay ends, you are not required to leave solely because that period expires. However, you must make the necessary arrangements for your continued stay, your ordinary visa where applicable, and migration registration.

Can you remain in Russia after your visa expires?

Yes, provided the MVD has accepted your application and required documents for an RVP, or your application for a VNZh, by the end of your permitted stay. Article 5(2) of Federal Law No. 115-FZ provides an exception to the obligation to leave solely because that period expires. This does not mean that the visa document itself is automatically extended.

Visa, permitted stay and migration registration

A visa specifies the conditions of entry and stay, including the number of entries. Your permitted stay determines how long you may lawfully remain in Russia. Migration registration records your place of stay and related information. Registration does not create an independent legal basis for remaining in the country.

Do not rely solely on the expiry date printed on your visa. Your permitted stay may be subject to a separate limit or shortened, for example when your studies end.

How to extend an ordinary visa after applying for residence

Russian Government Resolution No. 335 expressly provides for extending an ordinary visa while an RVP or VNZh application is being considered. To do this, the foreign national submits a written application to the MVD. A separate request from the inviting party is not required in this situation.

Apply before your permitted stay expires. Prepare your passport, visa, confirmation that your application has been accepted and other documents required under the applicable procedure. Check the state fee and processing arrangements.

Extending a visa on this basis does not convert a single-entry visa into a multiple-entry visa.

If you entered on an e-visa or without a visa

A unified electronic visa is subject to special restrictions. It can be extended only in exceptional circumstances prescribed by law, such as emergency medical treatment or force majeure. Do not assume the ordinary visa extension procedure applies to e-visas.

If your RVP or VNZh application has been accepted, the exception to the obligation to leave under Article 5 of Federal Law No. 115-FZ applies separately. Contact the MVD in advance to arrange your continued stay under the rules applicable to your entry status.

MVD Order No. 926 provides for a separate application to extend permitted stay. The required documents include your passport with copies of all pages and a copy of the confirmation of acceptance of your RVP or VNZh application. Visa-exempt foreign nationals should also check the applicable procedure.

How to update migration registration

Once arrangements for your continued stay have been completed, update your migration registration to reflect the new period. Where information specified by law changes or you remain at the same address beyond the originally declared period, the rules provide for notifying the MVD within three working days, by the host or by the foreign national in the prescribed cases.

Coordinate with your landlord or other host. Prepare the arrival notification, documents confirming the new period of stay and a written statement explaining the circumstances where required. Keep proof of submission.

Example: your Decree 702 visa is expiring

Suppose your single-entry private visa expires on 10 December and the MVD formally accepts your RVP documents on 20 November. You do not have to leave solely because your original permitted stay expires, but you should arrange your continued stay and visa documents in advance and then update migration registration.

If you only have an MVD appointment for 15 December, however, you cannot remain after 10 December without another lawful basis.

Can you leave Russia and return?

An accepted residence application does not replace the documents required for entry. Before travelling, check that you have a valid visa with an unused entry or another legal basis for returning. A single-entry visa that you have already used to enter Russia does not allow you to re-enter after departure. Also check whether your trip may affect the residence application process.

Can you work while your application is pending?

Acceptance of an RVP or VNZh application does not itself create a new right to work. Until your residence status is issued, employment is permitted only on the basis of valid documents or statutory exemptions. If you already hold an RVP, a temporary residence permit for educational purposes (RVPO), a work patent or a work permit, check its validity separately.

What if you encounter difficulties with the MVD?

Gather your passport, visa, confirmation of acceptance, proof of migration registration and records of your communications. Identify the precise problem: arranging your permitted stay, extending your visa, migration registration or acceptance of your application. If the matter cannot be resolved locally, you can submit a reasoned written request to the MVD and assess the available appeal procedures.

If your residence application is refused

After a refusal, do not assume that your right to remain continues indefinitely. Check the date you received the notification, your permitted stay and whether another lawful basis applies. Federal Law No. 115-FZ provides for challenging a refusal before the federal-level MVD within three working days or in court within three months of receiving the notification. The court appeal deadline does not itself allow you to remain in Russia for three months: your legal basis for staying and the availability of interim protective measures must be assessed separately.

Frequently asked questions

My visa expires tomorrow and my application has already been accepted. What should I do?

Obtain confirmation of acceptance and contact the MVD without delay to arrange your continued stay.

Does confirmation of acceptance replace a visa?

No. It confirms acceptance of the application but is not a document authorising re-entry.

Can the whole family remain if only one spouse applied?

One spouse's legal basis does not automatically extend to other family members. Check each person's status and the special rules applicable to children and legal representatives.

Key takeaway

Formal acceptance of an application before the end of permitted stay allows you to rely on the statutory exception to the obligation to leave. Make the necessary arrangements for your continued stay and do not confuse permission to stay with permission to re-enter.
