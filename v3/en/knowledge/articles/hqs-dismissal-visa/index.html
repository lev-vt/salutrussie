Highly qualified specialists in Russia: termination of employment, changing employers and residence rights

What a highly qualified specialist should do after termination: 30 working days to change employers, departure deadlines, visas, family residence permits and the new rules in 2027.

Lev VtiurinLawyer and founder of SalutRussie · In legal practice since 2005SalutRussie editorial teamLast reviewed: 24 November 2026

In brief. If the contract of a highly qualified specialist (HQS) is terminated early, the specialist has 30 working days to find a new employer and arrange a new work permit. If no new contract is concluded or the petition is rejected, and there is no other lawful basis for staying, a further 30 calendar days are allowed for departure. Different rules apply to indefinite permanent residence permits (VNZh). Here we explain what employees, their families and employers should do.

How long can you stay after termination?

If an HQS employment contract or civil-law services contract is terminated early, a special procedure applies under Article 13.2(11) of Federal Law No. 115-FZ.

PeriodWhat it means
30 working daysThe period for finding a new employer and arranging a new work permit following early termination of the contract.
Petition processing periodIf a new contract is concluded and a petition submitted in time, the existing documents remain valid while the petition is being considered.
30 calendar daysThe period allowed for departure if no new contract is concluded during the initial period or the Ministry of Internal Affairs (MVD) rejects the petition, and there is no other lawful basis for staying or residing in Russia.

The final period runs from the end of the job-search period or from the decision rejecting the petition. Describing the rule as 60 days after termination is therefore inaccurate. If a contract ends because its agreed term expires rather than through early termination, do not automatically assume that this special procedure applies.

What happens to your visa and work permit?

Subject to the conditions of the special procedure, the HQS work permit, visa and fixed-term VNZh remain valid, as do dependent visas and residence permits held by family members. These documents cease to be valid at the end of the prescribed departure period.

The continued validity of documents during the job-search period does not authorise you to start working for another company under your old permit. Your new employer must follow the prescribed HQS recruitment procedure. If you have an independent right to work, for example under an indefinite VNZh, assess it separately.

How to change employers

Start preparing before termination if possible. Check whether the new employer is entitled to hire highly qualified specialists, the contract terms and compliance with statutory remuneration criteria.

  1. Conclude a new employment contract or an eligible civil-law services contract.
  2. Arrange submission of the petition to the MVD within the prescribed job-search period and obtain confirmation of acceptance.
  3. Monitor the decision: the general processing period is no more than 14 working days from receipt of the petition by the MVD.
  4. Obtain the new work permit and arrange the necessary visa, VNZh and family documents.

A job offer, negotiations or a petition that has not been submitted do not extend the special regime. Do not leave the paperwork until the last day.

What if your new permit is approved but you have not collected it?

This is a separate situation. An HQS must apply to collect the new work permit within 30 calendar days of the MVD's decision. Where valid reasons exist and the employer submits a petition within the original period, later collection is possible within 30 calendar days of the MVD receiving that petition (Article 13.2(15) of Federal Law No. 115-FZ).

If the collection deadline is missed, the decision to issue the permit is revoked. The specialist and family members who have no other lawful grounds for staying must leave within 15 calendar days after the prescribed collection period expires. This is not the 30-day period applicable when no new contract is concluded or a petition is rejected.

What happens to your permanent residence permit after termination?

Type of permanent residence permitConsequences of termination
VNZh valid for the duration of the HQS work permitDepends on the special HQS regime. When changing employers, you must arrange a new VNZh under the prescribed procedure.
Indefinite VNZh obtained after two years of work as an HQSDoes not end automatically because of termination. General grounds for cancellation and permanent residence obligations apply.
VNZh obtained on another independent groundMust be assessed separately; termination does not in itself mean loss of this status.

To obtain an indefinite VNZh under the special HQS route, you must have worked in that capacity for at least two years, resided under an HQS residence permit, and the employer or client must have duly fulfilled its tax obligations. Family members may obtain indefinite residence permits if the statutory conditions are met.

If you are approaching the two-year threshold, assess whether you can obtain an indefinite VNZh before the contract ends. Completing two years of employment does not automatically convert a fixed-term document into an indefinite one.

How does termination affect your spouse and children?

Family members do not always have to leave with the HQS. If their visa or fixed-term VNZh was issued in connection with the specialist's employment, the special deadlines apply. If a family member holds an independent status, such as their own permanent residence permit, assess their position separately.

Check every family member's documents in advance, especially if your children attend school in Russia and your spouse is employed. If the petition relating to the new employer is approved, arrange the corresponding family documents.

New HQS requirements from 1 March 2027

When choosing a new employer, take into account the increase in minimum remuneration provided for by Federal Law No. 241-FZ.

CategoryFrom 1 March 2027
General HQS ruleRUB 717,000 per calendar month
Special categories expressly specified by lawRUB 358,500 per calendar month

Before that date, the minimum for most highly qualified specialists is RUB 750,000 per quarter; different conditions apply to special categories. Do not apply the new threshold uniformly to every worker without checking their category. Federal Law No. 241-FZ also provides for annual indexation of these thresholds based on the growth of average monthly accrued wages in the preceding year.

An important alternative. For foreign nationals who obtained an HQS work permit before 1 March 2027, Federal Law No. 241-FZ provides for switching to an ordinary work permit or work patent without leaving Russia. If the relevant conditions are met, a worker who requires a visa may obtain a multiple-entry work visa upon the employer's petition. This is a separate procedure, not an automatic change of status; assess the possibility before your lawful stay expires.

What must your former employer do?

The employer or client must notify the territorial MVD office of termination of the contract with a foreign national within three working days of the termination date. The employer also remains subject to applicable reporting obligations concerning remuneration paid to highly qualified specialists.

Request the contract termination document, a copy of the notification or evidence of its submission, and remuneration records. The job-search period runs from the date of termination, not the date on which the employer sends its notification.

What if you cannot find a new employer?

Before the job-search period ends, assess alternative lawful grounds: an independent VNZh, family or professional routes to residence, or switching to another employment regime. Owning property, having a Russian spouse or having a child does not itself replace a valid immigration document.

If no suitable alternative exists, prepare to leave within the special calendar-day deadline. Do not assume that an interview or a promise from a prospective employer extends your stay.

Frequently asked questions

Can I leave Russia and return on my old work visa?

Before travelling, check the visa's validity, number of entries, the grounds on which it remains valid and your permitted stay. The right to search for a new employer does not itself guarantee re-entry.

Can I stay if my new employer submits the petition on the last day?

The law provides for documents to remain valid during consideration of the petition if the conditions of the special procedure are met. You must be able to establish timely conclusion of the new contract and acceptance of the petition.

I have worked as an HQS for two years. Do I need to apply separately for indefinite permanent residence?

Yes. If the statutory conditions are met, you must apply for an indefinite VNZh; the fixed-term document does not become indefinite automatically.

Key takeaway

Termination of an HQS contract does not mean immediate loss of lawful stay. However, distinguish the job-search period, departure deadline and separate deadline for collecting a new work permit. Check your family's status and the legislative changes taking effect in 2027.
