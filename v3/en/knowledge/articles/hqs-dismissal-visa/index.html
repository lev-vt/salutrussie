PRACTICAL LEGAL GUIDE · Employment

Dismissal of a highly qualified specialist (HQS)

Editorial edition · 24 November 2026

In brief — Early termination of an HQS employment or civil contract can trigger a 30-working-day statutory period to find another qualifying employer and obtain a new permit under the prescribed procedure. A separate 30-calendar-day departure period may apply if no new contract is concluded or the petition is rejected and no other lawful basis exists.

Identify the triggering date

Obtain the signed termination document, permit and visa dates. The first period uses working days, not calendar days. Record family members' dependent status and any independent residence grounds.

New employer's duties

A job offer is not a new work permit. The new employer must be eligible and submit the prescribed petition and contract. Check the statutory procedure and confirm lawful stay and work rights during the transition. Keep submission and decision evidence.

2027 planning

New HQS remuneration requirements are scheduled for 1 March 2027. Check the applicable category and transitional rules before agreeing the salary. If no new lawful basis is secured, plan departure within the statutory deadline.

Legal basis and official sources

Article 13.2(11), Law No. 115-FZ; Law No. 241-FZ. Official publication portal · Federal Tax Service.
