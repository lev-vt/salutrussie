Renting an apartment in Russia as a foreign national: contracts, deposits and registration

A practical guide to renting a home in Russia: checking the owner, tenancy agreements, payments, deposits, early departure and migration registration.

Lev VtiurinLawyer and founder of SalutRussie · In legal practice since 2005SalutRussie editorial teamLast reviewed: 24 November 2026

In brief. Foreign nationals can rent accommodation in Russia. Before paying, check who is entitled to let the apartment, the proposed tenancy terms and how migration registration will be arranged. This guide takes you from finding a home to recovering your deposit.

Where to start looking for an apartment

First decide on the area, budget, length of stay and household composition. In addition to monthly rent, budget for utilities, a security deposit and any agent's fee.

If you are still outside Russia, consider arranging temporary accommodation initially so that you can inspect your long-term home before signing a tenancy agreement. Before booking, clarify how migration registration will be arranged. Do not transfer substantial sums solely on the strength of a listing, photographs or an agent's promise.

How to check the owner and the agent's authority

Residential accommodation may be let by the owner or someone authorised by the owner. Request identification and current information from the Unified State Register of Real Estate (EGRN). If the property has multiple owners, check their participation or consent to the letting.

Publicly available EGRN extracts provide limited information about individual owners' identities. Ask the owner for a recent extract containing their details and verify its authenticity, or use a legally permitted method of obtaining the information. A screenshot alone is not a substitute for verification.

If a representative signs the agreement, check evidence of their authority, including their right to sign the agreement and receive payments. For a subletting arrangement, review the original agreement and the landlord's consent. An agent showing the apartment is not necessarily authorised to let it or accept payment.

Example. An estate agent shows you an apartment, a relative of the owner proposes signing the agreement, and you are asked to transfer money to a third party. Pause payment until you have established each person's authority and the legal basis for the transfer.

What a tenancy agreement should contain

When an individual rents residential accommodation to live in, Russian law generally treats this as a residential tenancy agreement (dogovor naima), although it is commonly described as a rental agreement. It must be made in writing. Ask for a draft in advance, especially if you do not speak Russian.

Put important verbal promises into the agreement. International clients may benefit from a bilingual version specifying which language version prevails if there are discrepancies.

Why an 11-month agreement is not always better

A residential tenancy agreement may be concluded for up to five years. If no term is specified, it is generally deemed to be five years. For a term of at least one year, the resulting encumbrance on ownership must be registered in the EGRN.

Eleven-month agreements are common, but short-term tenancies have particular rules: some protections applicable to longer tenancies generally do not apply unless the parties agree otherwise. For example, do not automatically assume you have a preferential right to renew the tenancy.

Choose a term based on your plans and the agreement's terms, not solely on the owner's convenience.

Costs: rent, deposit and estate agent's commission

PaymentWhat you pay for
RentThe agreed period of occupation.
Security depositSecurity for contractual obligations, for example compensation for damage or arrears if provided for in the agreement.
Agent's commissionA separate fee for agreed intermediary services.

Clarify every payment, its recipient and its purpose. Do not assume that the deposit automatically covers the final month's rent: this must be agreed. If there are no contractual grounds for deductions, the security deposit is generally returned unless the parties have agreed otherwise.

Specify the repayment deadline, how damage is recorded, how expenses are substantiated and how arrears are calculated. Address normal wear and tear separately. If paying cash, obtain a signed receipt; for a bank transfer, specify the payment purpose and retain confirmation.

A unilateral rent increase is permitted only in cases provided for by law or the agreement. Check the wording of the rent adjustment provisions.

How to arrange migration registration in rented accommodation

Discuss registration before signing the agreement. Migration registration at your actual place of stay does not give you an ownership share in the apartment or replace a tenancy agreement.

The law permits foreign nationals to submit their own arrival notification under a tenancy agreement concluded with their host. In the prescribed cases, they can apply in person to the Ministry of Internal Affairs (MVD) or file electronically. Holders of a temporary residence permit (RVP) or permanent residence permit (VNZh) may need registration at their place of residence, while certain categories in Moscow and the Moscow Region are subject to a special pilot scheme.

Agree on the provision of the owner's required documents and details of any children living in the apartment. A signed agreement alone does not confirm that migration registration has been completed and does not extend a visa.

What to do when collecting the keys

Sign a property handover record. Record the move-in date, the condition of the apartment, furniture and appliances, existing damage, meter readings and the number of keys. Take photographs and agree them with the landlord.

Check locks, heating, water supply and appliances. Agree whom to contact in an emergency. As a general rule, routine repairs are the tenant's responsibility and major repairs are the landlord's, unless the agreement provides otherwise. Set out the allocation of responsibilities in advance.

How to end the tenancy and recover your deposit

As a general rule, the tenant may terminate the agreement by giving the landlord three months' written notice, with the consent of other permanent occupants. The parties may agree a more convenient termination procedure, including an agreement for early departure. Check the terms before signing.

Termination at the landlord's request generally requires court proceedings. Short-term tenancies may include contractual grounds for out-of-court termination, but these cannot be arbitrary: the Russian Constitutional Court requires a clearly defined material violation of the landlord's legitimate interests attributable to the tenant. Termination of the agreement and forced eviction are separate matters: following termination of a residential tenancy agreement, forced eviction requires a court decision (Article 688 of the Russian Civil Code).

When moving out, sign a return handover record, document the condition of the apartment and meter readings, obtain confirmation of the return of keys and agree the final settlement. If the deposit is withheld, request the reasons and supporting documents. If a dispute arises, retain the agreement, handover records, photographs and payment confirmations.

Frequently asked questions

Does the tenancy agreement need to be notarised?

Generally, no. The law requires written form; for a term of at least one year, consider the rules on state registration of the encumbrance.

Can I conclude an agreement before arriving in Russia?

You can agree the terms and execute an agreement remotely, provided the parties are properly identified and written form requirements are met. Verify the apartment, the signatories' authority and key handover arrangements before paying.

Will the tenancy end if the apartment is sold?

As a general rule, a change of ownership does not terminate or alter the tenancy agreement: the new owner becomes the landlord. For a particular agreement, check its validity and the required formalities.

What if the owner refuses to provide documents for migration registration?

Discuss this before paying. If the agreement has already been concluded, check whether you can file independently under the agreement and obtain the necessary documents. Do not register at a fictitious address.

Key takeaway

Safe renting starts with checking the owner, authority and contract terms, not with paying a deposit. For foreign nationals, arranging migration registration in advance is equally important.
