Renting a home in Russia: legal checks

Editorial edition · 24 November 2026

SalutRussie editorial teamFounder in legal practice since 2005Editorial version: 24 November 2026

In brief — Before transferring money, establish who owns the property, who is authorized to sign and how migration registration will be completed. A signed lease is not proof of completed registration.

Check the parties and property

Request evidence of ownership, the representative's authority, and a draft agreement. Compare the address and the owner or signatory with the supporting documents. If an agent requests payment to a third person, clarify the recipient and purpose in writing.

Agree the payment and handover terms

Record rent, utilities, deposit, return conditions, early termination and repairs. Use an inventory and handover report with meter readings. Keep payment evidence and copies of correspondence.

Migration registration

Identify the lawful receiving party and applicable notification deadline. A foreign tenant may have a statutory self-notification route under Article 22(3.5) of Law 109-FZ where the conditions are met; retain proof of actual submission.

Legal basis and official sources

Civil Code; Federal Law No. 109-FZ. Official publication portal · Federal Tax Service.
