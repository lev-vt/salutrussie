Temporary residence under Decree 702: eligibility, documents, deadlines and changes in 2027

How to move to Russia under Decree 702: eligibility for the Shared Values programme, obtaining a visa and temporary residence, required documents and changes in 2027.

Lev VtiurinLawyer and founder of SalutRussie · In legal practice since 2005SalutRussie editorial teamLast reviewed: 24 November 2026

In brief. Russian Presidential Decree No. 702 allows certain foreign nationals to obtain a temporary residence permit (RVP) without a quota or proof of knowledge of Russian language, history and law. A special three-month, single-entry private visa may be issued for entry. The exemptions do not remove identity and document checks, grounds for refusal or lawful stay requirements.

Important clarification. Decree No. 702 of 19 August 2024 provides for a three-month, single-entry ordinary private visa; check the actual dates printed on your visa. Distinguish applying through a consulate from applying in Russia. If you apply through a consulate, medical examinations take place after arrival in Russia, generally within 30 calendar days (Article 6(4.2) of Federal Law No. 115-FZ, as amended from 1 September 2026). Where valid reasons for delay are documented, a later deadline applies, no more than 30 calendar days after those circumstances cease. From 1 January 2027, RVP holders under this route are exempt from the new ground for refusal and cancellation under Article 7(1)(8); annual notifications and other requirements remain applicable. Federal Law No. 241-FZ of 26 July 2026.

The most expensive mistake is buying tickets and ordering certificates before discovering that you have chosen the wrong legal route or cannot use a particular document for your application.

What the programme offers — and what it does not

What it offersWhat it does not offer
An opportunity to apply for an RVP without a quota if you meet the conditions of the DecreeAutomatic approval of your RVP
Exemption from the specific knowledge requirement at the RVP stage under this routeExemption from all requirements when subsequently applying for permanent residence or citizenship
A special visa route for applying for an RVPAn unrestricted right to travel in and out of Russia while your application is pending
A basis for planning further lawful residenceAn immediate right to work in any region merely by submitting an application

An RVP is generally issued for three years. It is a status of temporary residence, not an indefinite permanent residence permit. After obtaining it, you have separate obligations relating to registration at your place of residence, annual residence notifications, employment rules and limits on time spent abroad.

Who qualifies under Decree 702?

The Decree applies to foreign nationals and stateless persons wishing to move to Russia from their country of nationality or permanent residence for the reasons set out in paragraph 1 of the Decree. Both nationality and permanent residence are therefore relevant. For example, if you hold a passport from a country outside the list but have permanent residence rights in a listed country, you must check whether this route applies and document the relevant circumstances. The special procedure uses the list of countries and territoriesapproved by Russian Government Order No. 2560-r of 17 September 2024.

Before starting, check four things:

  1. All nationalities, permanent residence rights abroad and documents you intend to use to enter Russia and apply. If you hold multiple nationalities, do not choose a passport automatically.
  2. Whether the Decree applies to you and your intended entry route.
  3. Your current immigration status: whether you are outside Russia, already in Russia on a visa or have another basis for staying.
  4. Whether any circumstances could prevent an RVP from being granted regardless of the programme.

Important: family circumstances and agreement with the values alone do not replace verification of the conditions of the special procedure. Obtaining a Decree 702 visa does not mean your RVP has been approved.

Is Decree 702 the right route for you?

The programme is not always the quickest or most stable route. If you already have a Russian spouse, a child who is a Russian citizen, a degree from a Russian university or another special ground, first compare the available routes to temporary and permanent residence. One route may lead directly to a permanent residence permit (VNZh), while another leads only to an RVP. Documents, timelines and post-approval obligations differ.

Choose your legal route before paying for notarised translations or tickets. At this stage, you can still avoid unnecessary costs by selecting a different route.

The visa and residence permit: two separate stages

Official consular guidance provides for a three-month, single-entry ordinary private visa (some consular guidance describes it as "up to 90 days"; check the actual visa dates). It is intended for entry followed by an RVP application.

Whether you must leave Russia first to obtain a special visa if you are already staying lawfully cannot be decided on the basis of someone else's experience on Telegram. Assess the Decree and application procedure against your actual immigration status and legal basis for staying. Leaving to obtain a new visa without checking alternatives can create additional costs and a risk that you cannot return.

What to check before travelling

  • Which Russian consular district you can apply in and how to book a visa appointment.
  • What form of evidence the particular consulate requires to establish the relevant circumstances.
  • Entry arrangements for your spouse and children: a decision relating to one family member does not automatically apply to the others.
  • Who will provide your actual accommodation address and arrange migration registration in Russia.
  • Whether you can obtain the required RVP documents within the visa period.

What costs should you budget for?

There is no single programme cost that can be quoted without knowing your nationality and family circumstances. Divide your budget into categories:

  1. Government fees: consular fees and state duties at the applicable rates, taking any exemptions into account.
  2. Foreign documents: certificates, extracts, apostilles or consular legalisation, and international delivery.
  3. Documents in Russia: translation, notarisation, medical examinations and photographs.
  4. Logistics: travel, accommodation and visits to the MVD; repeat visits may add substantial costs.
  5. Legal support: a separate service that is not legally mandatory and does not guarantee a government decision.

Compare service providers by their scope of work: eligibility assessment, application preparation, document review, filing assistance and action in the event of refusal. A promise of a fixed-price turnkey RVP does not tell you whether certificates, notarial fees and travel are included.

Preparing documents without paying twice

The precise list depends on the MVD regulations, nationality, age and place of application. In practice, it is helpful to prepare documents in the following order.

Practical rule: do not pay to translate a foreign certificate until you have established what authentication of the original is required for recognition in Russia. Apostilles, notarised translations and electronic QR codes serve different purposes.

Why applications are returned over small discrepancies

One passport spells the name Alex, an old visa spells it Aleks, and a notarised translation uses Aleksandr. This is not always a legal problem, but the MVD must be able to identify the same person across all documents. Check given names, surnames, dates of birth, document numbers and translation details before booking an appointment. If there is a material discrepancy, obtain supporting evidence rather than altering the translation at your own discretion.

Submitting your application and awaiting a decision

  1. Check your eligibility. Prepare a short assessment of why the Decree applies, any refusal risks and the appropriate region for applying.
  2. Prepare your documents. Authenticate foreign documents and check the current MVD requirements.
  3. Arrange entry and accommodation. Do not confuse a rental agreement with completed migration registration.
  4. Submit your application. Obtain documentary confirmation that the application has been accepted for consideration, not merely an appointment ticket.
  5. Check your permitted stay. If your visa is expiring, separately address lawful stay, visa documentation and migration registration.
  6. Await the decision and obtain your status. Check how to obtain your RVP, register afterwards and exercise your right to work.

Under Article 5(2) of Federal Law No. 115-FZ, if the RVP application and required documents have been accepted by the end of the foreign national's permitted stay, they need not leave solely because that period expires. Arrange documentation of your continued stay and migration registration with the MVD in advance. However, this does not convert an expired single-entry visa into a multiple-entry visa. Departure and re-entry require a separate assessment.

Planning your move: before arrival, after arrival and after the decision

Before travelling

First, prepare a document checklist showing the country of issue, time needed to obtain each document, authentication requirements and expiry dates. Criminal record certificates deserve particular attention: for certain visa applicants and RVP applications, both their contents and recognition by the Russian authority matter. Consulates and the MVD may impose requirements at different stages. Do not order a supposedly universal package based on the experience of a blogger who applied in another country.

Next, choose a Russian region. This is not simply a matter of finding the nearest airport. You need to live there in practice, access the application procedure and consider the regional restrictions on future employment under an RVP. If your family intends to live in Moscow but you plan to apply in another region because appointments are easier to obtain, assess the consequences separately.

After arrival

During the first few days, comply with applicable migration registration rules and establish which medical documents, translations and confirmations are required at the particular office under the applicable regulations. Booking an appointment is only an administrative step. What matters legally is whether your application and required documents have been accepted. Retain proof of acceptance, the date, the name of the authority and a list of the documents submitted.

Distinguish the stages: lawful stay in Russia on a valid visa, submitting an application and obtaining an RVP are three different situations. Each transition requires its own evidence.

After a positive decision

Check how to obtain the residence endorsement or document, register at your place of residence, meet notification deadlines and, where possible, obtain a multiple-entry travel document. Separately plan the timing of your permanent residence application, particularly if you intend to work, run a business or spend extended periods outside Russia.

Three situations where legal advice is particularly useful

Your criminal record certificate is ready but the MVD will not accept it. First establish the precise reason: format, country of issue, apostille, date or translation. Do not reorder every document before identifying the problem.

Your RVP application is pending but your visa is about to expire. We check the date of formal acceptance, valid documents, migration registration and the MVD's actions. Simply extending registration is not a substitute for legal analysis.

You urgently need to return home. We separately establish the basis on which you can re-enter Russia. Awaiting an RVP decision does not guarantee the right to return.

Four mistakes that are particularly difficult to correct

Using the wrong passport when you hold two nationalities. Entry conditions, visas and identity evidence may differ between passports. Using one passport for entry and another for your application requires separate assessment; do not change documents midway without understanding the consequences.

Obtaining a certificate that does not meet document recognition requirements. Some countries issue electronic certificates with online authenticity checks. However, a QR code does not always replace an apostille, legalisation or notarial confirmation where required. First establish the applicable authentication procedure in writing.

Overestimating the effect of submitting an application. Even if applying allows you to remain lawfully pending a decision under Article 5 of Federal Law No. 115-FZ, it does not automatically grant a new visa, a work permit or re-entry rights. Assess each document separately when planning travel.

Overlooking the consequences of a refusal. A refusal may arise from different circumstances: procedural violations, unsuitable documents or grounds preventing the status from being granted. The precise legal ground for the decision determines your next step, not a general statement that the MVD rejected the documents.

After obtaining your RVP: plan the next stage

An RVP is subject to a specific legal regime and is generally tied to a particular Russian region. Under Article 13(5) of Federal Law No. 115-FZ, a temporary resident's employment outside the region where residence is authorised is restricted unless a specific exception applies. Check any plan to obtain an RVP in one region and work in another in advance.

Record the dates of RVP issuance, registration, annual notifications and your planned permanent residence application. Under the standard route, you may apply for permanent residence after eight months of the first year of residence under an RVP, but no later than four months before the RVP expires. Permanent residence can be granted after one year of residence under an RVP (Article 8 of Federal Law No. 115-FZ). Other rules apply where an independent legal ground exists. Annual RVP notifications must be submitted within two months after each successive year from the date you received your permit (Article 6(9)).

Changes in 2027. From 1 February 2027, Federal Law No. 314-FZ allows holders of an RVP under Decree 702 to apply again on the same ground between six and four months before their existing permit expires. This is a new application, not an automatic renewal. From the same date, Article 15.4 of Federal Law No. 115-FZ introduces criminal record certificate requirements: generally, a paper document no more than three months old from each country of nationality and permanent residence. Exceptions apply to certain categories, including children under 14 and some people who previously submitted such certificates. Check the requirements applicable on the filing date before ordering certificates.

Short answers to additional questions

Can I apply for an RVP at a Russian consulate in advance?

Yes. You may submit an RVP application through a Russian diplomatic mission or consulate in your country of residence (Article 6(4) of Federal Law No. 115-FZ). The decision is made by the territorial MVD office in Russia, not the consulate. The standard four-month decision period under the visa procedure runs from the date the application reaches the territorial MVD office. Medical examinations, fingerprinting and photography take place in Russia under the applicable procedure.

Do I have to live with the person who invited me?

The visa entry route and actual accommodation address must be assessed separately. Migration registration concerns your real place of stay, not an address used merely for convenience.

Can I work while my application is pending?

Acceptance of an RVP application does not itself confer employment rights associated with temporary residence. Until the RVP is issued, your existing status and independent right to work must be assessed.

What if my family circumstances change before the decision?

Inform your lawyer or the competent authority of material changes and check whether new documents are needed. A child's birth, passport replacement, a surname change and a change of address are particularly important.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to prove knowledge of Russian?

The relevant proof is not required when applying for an RVP under the Decree 702 exemption. Other procedures have their own rules.

Can the whole family apply together?

You can plan a joint family strategy, but eligibility and documents are assessed separately for each applicant.

How long does the process take?

The standard decision period for the visa route is four months from receipt by the territorial MVD office (Article 6(4) of Federal Law No. 115-FZ). A separate period of up to 60 days applies to the visa-exempt route if the conditions of Article 6.1 are met. Preparing certificates, obtaining a visa, booking appointments and obtaining the residence document after approval take additional time.

Does an RVP refusal mean the programme has closed?

No. Establish the grounds for refusal and assess appeal options. Following refusal or cancellation of an RVP, a new application under the same procedure can generally be made no earlier than one year later (Article 6(6) of Federal Law No. 115-FZ). Distinguish refusal to accept an application package from a decision refusing to grant an RVP.

Key takeaway

Decree 702 is a genuine preferential route to temporary residence, but a successful application depends not on an appealing relocation story but on the correct legal grounds, acceptable documents and compliance with immigration deadlines. You can prepare an application yourself; legal review is especially useful before filing or taking irreversible steps such as leaving Russia, changing status or submitting an incomplete package.
