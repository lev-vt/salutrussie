Temporary residence under Decree No. 702

Editorial edition · 24 November 2026

In brief — Eligible foreign nationals can apply for a temporary residence permit (RVP) outside the quota and without the usual language, history and law certificate. Eligibility and general refusal grounds still need to be checked. The decree does not guarantee a permit.

Eligibility and entry

Confirm the applicable country list, the relevant nationality or permanent residence, and the grounds for moving under the decree. The special ordinary private visa is single-entry for three months under the decree; check the actual dates with the consulate. A visa is not an RVP. Where filing through a consulate is available, check the separate submission procedure and medical deadlines.

Document and filing plan

Obtain the required criminal record document, apostille or legalisation where applicable, and correct translations. Arrange medical examinations and fingerprinting under the applicable procedure. File with the competent authority and retain confirmation of acceptance. A booking or informal visit is not proof that an application was accepted.

After the decision

Check registration, regional employment rules and annual notification. From 1 January 2027, the new RVP income-related cancellation ground has an express exception for this programme; annual notification and other obligations remain. Check VNZh eligibility separately.

Legal basis and official sources

Presidential Decree No. 702 of 19 August 2024; Law No. 115-FZ. Official publication portal · Federal Tax Service.
