Annual residence notification: the 2027 income changes

Editorial edition · 24 November 2026

In brief — RVP and VNZh holders normally file a residence notification within two months after each completed year of residence calculated from receipt of the permit. The rules differ; new income-related provisions take effect on 1 January 2027.

Calculate your own deadline

Use the date of receipt of the permit, not the calendar year-end. Establish the reporting year and due date. Check the correct authority and accepted submission channel. For permanent residence, every fifth year has a specific personal-submission requirement.

Income versus savings

Distinguish legally recognised income from an account balance, transfers and informal support. Collect employment or business records, tax documents and other evidence permitted for your status. Law 241-FZ introduces new conditions and evidence rules; review exceptions and transitional provisions before the reporting period.

Decree 702 and other exceptions

RVP holders under Decree 702 are expressly excepted from the new income-related RVP cancellation ground. That does not abolish annual notification, other cancellation grounds or separate VNZh requirements. Review the exact legal ground of your permit before relying on any exception.

Legal basis and official sources

Articles 6–9 of Law No. 115-FZ; Federal Law No. 241-FZ of 26 July 2026. Official publication portal · Federal Tax Service.
