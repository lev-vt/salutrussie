Annual residence notifications in Russia: deadlines, documents and income rules from 2027

How to submit annual notifications for temporary and permanent residence, calculate deadlines, prove income and prepare for the rules from 1 January 2027.

Lev VtiurinLawyer and founder of SalutRussie · In legal practice since 2005SalutRussie editorial teamLast reviewed: 24 November 2026

In brief. If you hold a temporary residence permit (RVP) or permanent residence permit (VNZh), you must confirm your residence in Russia every year. The usual deadline is two months after the anniversary of receiving your permit. From 1 January 2027, income documentation requirements and grounds for cancellation change. Establish in advance which rules apply to you.

For holders of an RVP under Decree 702: from 1 January 2027, an exemption applies to the new ground for refusing or cancelling temporary residence relating to employment and income. Annual notifications remain mandatory; documents concerning the types of income specified by law must be submitted where such income is received. This exemption does not automatically continue when you obtain permanent residence.

First, identify the rules applicable to your status

From 1 January 2027, RVP notifications must be filed at the place of residence or, if none, the place of stay within the region where residence is authorised, subject to statutory exceptions.

Why temporary and permanent residence notifications differ

Different rules apply to RVP and VNZh holders. Use the form and filing procedure appropriate to your status. Prepare your passport, residence document and information on your accommodation, employment, trips outside Russia and income. If filing electronically, check document and acceptance confirmation requirements.

VNZh: after every fifth year of permanent residence, you must submit the notification personally to the territorial MVD office. Postal and electronic submissions do not replace personal attendance in that year.

How to calculate your filing deadline

The deadline is calculated from the date you actually received your RVP or VNZh, not the beginning of the calendar year. Keep the document confirming the date of receipt.

EventDate
VNZh received15 April 2026
End of the first year15 April 2027
Ordinary filing deadline15 June 2027
Latest filing date where valid reasons are documented15 October 2027

The final date is not an ordinary extension. If you miss the two-month deadline, you must document a valid reason. Do not leave filing until the last day.

Three situations: first notification, fifth year and passport replacement

First notification. If you received your permit in August 2026, submit your first notification after the corresponding date in August 2027. For certain types of income, you will need information for the 2026 calendar year.

Fifth VNZh notification. Once the fifth year of permanent residence has ended, you must attend the MVD in person.

Replacing your passport or VNZh document. Replacing the document does not itself start a new year of residence. If your status ended and was granted again, check the deadlines separately.

What changes on 1 January 2027?

From 1 January 2027, new rules on documentary evidence of income apply to RVP and VNZh holders. The law distinguishes two periods: income for the relevant year of residence and income for the preceding calendar year.

Document or income categoryPeriod

If you worked for several employers, you must substantiate the combined income from the relevant employment. Special rules apply to certain categories and to children.

Is money in a bank account sufficient?

From 2027, the balance of your savings does not replace the income documents specified by law. Interest received from a Russian bank may qualify if statutory requirements are met. However, interest income does not necessarily remove a separate requirement to undertake employment or business activity.

How to calculate the required income

For people subject to the new income adequacy criterion, the calculation uses the regional per capita subsistence minimum multiplied by the prescribed regional coefficient, for the foreign national and each dependent family member in Russia. If no coefficient is set, or it is below one, a coefficient of one applies. The law also caps the increased minimum at the average accrued salary in the region.

Illustrative example: if the applicable minimum after adjustment is RUB 30,000 and the calculation includes the foreign national and two dependants, the indicative requirement is RUB 90,000 per month on average. This is an illustration only, not an actual rate for any region.

Check the applicable regional figure, dependants, nature of your activities and income periods. Do not apply future rules to notifications filed in 2026.

What information to prepare before the deadline

Do not wait until your anniversary to gather documents. Keep a simple table recording the period, status, place of residence, trips abroad, income sources, supporting documents, filing date and delivery method.

The distinction between the year of residence under your permit and the calendar tax year is particularly important for tax returns. Do not assume that a tax return for the preceding calendar year fully proves the required figures for a different reporting period.

Employment and income requirements: who qualifies for exemptions?

From 1 January 2027, the law introduces new grounds for refusing or cancelling RVP and VNZh status relating to the absence of prescribed employment or business activity, insufficient qualifying income and, in specific cases, failure to pay fixed advance personal income tax. These are separate conditions: having money or a tax return does not itself resolve the activity requirement.

Who qualifies for exemptions?

Exemptions include pensioners, full-time students at qualifying Russian educational or research institutions, persons recognised as incapable of work and certain categories of family members. An exemption is also expressly provided for RVP holders under Decree 702. The categories and conditions differ between RVP and VNZh holders. Certain categories must submit income documents when they receive qualifying income, while the notification obligation remains.

A special rule also applies when changing employers: if employment ends during the reporting period and new employment begins within three months within that period, the status is not cancelled on this ground provided the income requirement for the employment period is met. Certain graduates granted permanent residence are subject to a special rule concerning employment after issuance.

Do not conclude that you will lose your status solely because of one circumstance, such as not being employed. First check the applicable ground and exemptions.

Income, savings and incoming payments: the legal distinction

Employment income is generally evidenced by employer documents, tax information and the relevant payments. Sole trader income requires assessment of the tax regime and what the tax return or bank statement actually proves. Interest on deposits is a separate category; a large account balance does not mean that an equally large amount of interest has been earned.

Transfers into your own account, repayment of money previously lent, gifts and proceeds from selling property may be subject to different legal rules. Do not add every incoming payment to the income reported to the MVD without checking the documentary requirements prescribed by law. For disputed income sources, prepare a legal justification for including them or avoid relying on unsuitable evidence.

How to prepare in practice

  1. Record the date you received your permit, the anniversary and the last day of the ordinary filing period.
  2. Identify the applicable version of the law: for example, notifications filed in 2026 and those filed after 1 January 2027 may be governed by different rules.
  3. Gather information on every income source for the required period: employment contracts, income certificates, sole trader records, tax returns and bank documents confirming interest.
  4. Distinguish income from transfers of your own funds. Depositing cash into an account does not constitute earnings.
  5. Check your dependants and region: the new criteria are not universal.
  6. Choose an eligible filing method, and keep proof of acceptance, postal evidence or electronic confirmation.
  7. If you have missed the deadline, document the valid reason and check the special procedure for late filing.

What to do if you miss the deadline

If you missed the two-month deadline for a valid reason, you may submit the notification no later than six months after the end of the relevant year of residence. Submit evidence of why timely filing was impossible together with the other mandatory documents.

If you also missed that deadline, do not wait for the next anniversary. Establish the date of the violation, gather evidence and seek legal assistance. Administrative penalties and other consequences may apply, depending on your status, circumstances and the particular grounds involved. For VNZh holders, continuously failing to submit notifications for any two calendar years after receiving the permit is an independent ground for cancellation.

Moving from temporary to permanent residence

If your VNZh is issued around the time your next RVP notification is due, do not automatically assume the earlier notification is no longer needed. You must check the date your former status ended, the end of its reporting year and the date you received your VNZh. The answer depends on the actual dates, not a general rule inferred by analogy.

Additional answers to common questions

Is a bank certificate showing a balance of RUB 500,000 sufficient?

A balance alone is not enough to determine this. Under the rules taking effect in 2027, you must check the types of qualifying income and permitted documents. An account balance and interest received on a deposit are not the same thing.

Can a relative submit my notification for me?

The filing method depends on your status, the applicable procedure and the year of residence. Take particular care in years when personal attendance is mandatory for VNZh holders. A notarised power of attorney does not override an express requirement to file personally.

Do I need to submit a notification if I spent six months abroad?

Do not draw conclusions solely from the number of days. Periods abroad may have independent consequences for your residence status and must be reported accurately where required. Check both the notification obligation and whether the extended absence is permissible.

Frequently asked questions

Can I submit my notification by post?

Postal filing is permitted for VNZh holders in applicable cases, but personal attendance is required after every fifth year of permanent residence. The RVP procedure differs and must be checked separately.

My employer will not provide an income certificate. What should I do?

Request records of payments and deductions, check whether your income can be substantiated through tax information and employment documents, and retain evidence of your request to the employer.

Can I use sole trader income?

Business activity may be relevant, but its actual conduct, documented amounts, reporting period and applicable rules must be considered.

What if I had no income?

Under the new rules, this may create a significant risk. Do not file an empty notification without assessing possible grounds for cancellation and applicable exemptions.

Key takeaway

The annual notification is no longer simply a matter of submitting a form to the MVD. Particularly from 2027, it becomes a check on the sustainability of your immigration status through documented, legally qualifying income. It is better to plan your deadlines and review your documents before the reporting year ends than to resolve problems after filing.
