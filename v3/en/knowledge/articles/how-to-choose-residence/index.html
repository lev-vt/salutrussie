How to move to Russia legally: choosing your route to residence

How to choose an immigration strategy in Russia: visas, temporary and permanent residence, Decree 702, family, repatriation, education, employment and investment.

Lev VtiurinLawyer and founder of SalutRussie · In legal practice since 2005SalutRussie editorial teamLast reviewed: 24 November 2026

In brief. There is no single immigration route for everyone. Start by checking your eligibility: in some cases, you can apply directly for a permanent residence permit (VNZh) without first obtaining an ordinary temporary residence permit (RVP). In other situations, temporary residence or an education or employment route may be more suitable.

Where to start: a temporary visit or relocation?

First establish why you want to come to Russia and for how long. A short exploratory visit, employment under a contract, studying and relocating your family permanently require different solutions.

Visas and visa-free entry are ways to stay temporarily and lawfully, not long-term residence statuses. As a general rule, visa-exempt foreign nationals may stay for a total of 90 days per calendar year, unless an exception applies under legislation or an international treaty, or their stay has been extended under the prescribed procedure. Leaving and re-entering Russia does not reset this annual limit.

If you intend to move long term, first check your eligibility for temporary or permanent residence, then choose how to enter Russia.

Temporary and permanent residence involve registration obligations, annual residence notifications and compliance with limits on time spent outside Russia. Permanent residence is not equivalent to citizenship.

Can you obtain permanent residence without temporary residence?

Yes. The law provides independent grounds for applying for a VNZh without first obtaining an RVP. If you qualify for one of these, compare both routes before starting: temporary residence may be an unnecessary intermediate stage.

Under the standard route through temporary residence, you may apply for a VNZh after eight months of the first year of residence and no later than four months before the RVP expires. A VNZh may be granted under this route after one year of residence under an RVP.

If no special ground for direct permanent residence applies, check eligibility for an RVP without a quota, or otherwise the possibility of obtaining a quota. Graduates holding an RVPO have a separate route to permanent residence after completing qualifying studies.

How to choose a route for the whole family

Spouses and children may have different nationalities, grounds for entry and eligibility for temporary or permanent residence. Check each person's status even if everyone plans to arrive together.

Example. One spouse has Russian ancestry and may qualify for direct permanent residence, while the other is considering Decree 702. First identify available family-based routes and the conditions of each option, then coordinate entry and filing dates. Not everyone needs to obtain the same status.

What to consider if you are moving in 2027

From 1 January 2027, income documentation requirements and grounds for refusal and cancellation of residence statuses change. The law provides exemptions for certain categories. An exemption when obtaining temporary residence does not necessarily continue when moving to permanent residence.

Compare not only how straightforward the application is, but also your ability to meet obligations after approval: working or running a business, documenting income, submitting notifications and complying with residence rules.

What information to prepare before a consultation

To choose an immigration strategy, briefly describe your circumstances:

  • Your nationality, any additional nationalities and countries of permanent residence.
  • Where you are now, your current legal basis for staying and when your permitted stay ends.
  • Your family circumstances, relatives' nationalities and any Russian ancestry.
  • Your education, occupation and plans for employment, business or investment.
  • Your intended region of residence, relocation timeline and any previous immigration decisions.

Please do not send passport details or other sensitive documents in your first message. If needed, we will agree how to share them separately.

Frequently asked questions

Can I obtain permanent residence by buying an apartment?

Buying property alone does not confer permanent residence. To qualify under the investor route, you must meet the specific criteria established by the Russian Government.

Do I have to obtain a special Decree 702 visa first?

No. The Decree itself does not make the special visa a mandatory condition for applying for an RVP. If you are already staying lawfully in Russia, check whether you can apply under your current status.

Does an MVD appointment guarantee the right to stay after my visa expires?

No. An appointment with the Ministry of Internal Affairs (MVD) is not the same as formal acceptance of your application. When your permitted stay is about to end, check the lawful grounds and procedure for arranging your continued stay.

Key takeaway

Choose your legal route first, then arrange your entry, prepare the documents and submit your application. A sound immigration strategy considers not only how to obtain residence status but also how to live in Russia in accordance with your plans once you have it.
