Choosing a legal route to live in Russia

Editorial edition · 24 November 2026

SalutRussie editorial teamFounder in legal practice since 2005Editorial version: 24 November 2026

In brief — A visa, a temporary residence permit (RVP) and a permanent residence permit (VNZh) serve different purposes. Start with your citizenship, present status, intended region, family circumstances and reasons for moving; do not select a route simply because someone else succeeded.

Compare your options

A visa governs entry and stay. An RVP is normally valid for three years and involves regional restrictions. A VNZh provides permanent residence under its own statutory conditions. Some grounds allow direct access to VNZh; Decree No. 702 is a special RVP route, not an automatic permanent residence permit.

Build a personal timeline

Record the dates of every current visa, registration, permit and pending application. Check each family member separately. A booked appointment is not acceptance of an application and may not extend lawful stay. Confirm the relevant authority and documentary requirements before paying for translations or tickets.

When to obtain advice

Seek advice before an impending expiry, a new application following a refusal, or a change of employer or family status. Retain the written decisions and dates of receipt.

Legal basis and official sources

Federal Law No. 115-FZ. Official publication portal · Federal Tax Service.
