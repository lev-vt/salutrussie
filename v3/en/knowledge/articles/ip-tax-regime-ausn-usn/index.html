USN or AUSN: choosing a tax regime in Russia

Editorial edition · 24 November 2026

In brief — Choosing between the simplified system (USN) and automated simplified system (AUSN) requires more than comparing rates. Review eligibility, revenue, staff, expense structure, VAT and banking.

USN in 2026

The standard USN rates are generally 6% on revenue or 15% on revenue less deductible expenses, subject to applicable regional rules. From 2026, VAT exemption and liability depend on the statutory threshold and conditions, including the 20 million rouble threshold. The general VAT rate is 22%; special 5% or 7% rates may apply under conditions. Check the actual tax year and transactions.

AUSN conditions

AUSN generally uses 8% on revenue or 20% on revenue less expenses, with a minimum tax of 3% for the latter option. Key limits include annual income of 60 million roubles and no more than five employees, alongside other statutory requirements, eligible bank arrangements and regional availability.

Compare the whole cost

Model deductible expenses, VAT, contributions, payment channels and administrative cost. Check eligibility and notification deadlines with the Federal Tax Service. Do not assume the 2026 thresholds apply unchanged in 2027.

Legal basis and official sources

Tax Code, Chapter 26.2; Federal Law No. 17-FZ on AUSN. Official publication portal · Federal Tax Service.
