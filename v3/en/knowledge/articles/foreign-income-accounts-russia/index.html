Foreign income and overseas accounts when living in Russia

Editorial edition · 24 November 2026

SalutRussie editorial teamFounder in legal practice since 2005Editorial version: 24 November 2026

In brief — Tax residence, currency-law residence and migration status are different concepts. A Russian RVP or VNZh is not enough by itself to determine tax treatment of every receipt from abroad.

Tax residence and income

Calculate the relevant days in Russia and verify the final tax status for the tax period. The general tax-residence test is at least 183 calendar days during 12 consecutive months, subject to statutory rules. Classify each receipt by source and type; transfers of your own funds are not automatically taxable income. Consider treaty provisions and their current applicability.

Overseas accounts

Currency-law residence uses different criteria, including Russian permanent residence for foreign nationals. Check whether account opening, closure and movement reports are required and whether statutory exceptions apply. Do not assume every foreign account is subject to identical reporting.

Prepare evidence

Keep bank statements, contracts, tax certificates and proof of the nature and source of funds. Where self-declaration is required, the general 3-NDFL filing deadline is 30 April of the following year, subject to calendar adjustments and exceptions. A separate migration income requirement may apply.

Legal basis and official sources

Tax Code, Articles 207–209, 228–229; Federal Law No. 173-FZ. Official publication portal · Federal Tax Service.
