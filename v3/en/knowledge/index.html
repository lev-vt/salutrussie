Choosing a legal route to live in Russia

A visa, a temporary residence permit (RVP) and a permanent residence permit (VNZh) serve different purposes. Start with your citizenship, present status, intended region, family circumstances and reasons for moving; do not select a route simply because someone else succeeded.

Renting a home in Russia: legal checks

Before transferring money, establish who owns the property, who is authorized to sign and how migration registration will be completed. A signed lease is not proof of completed registration.

Starting a business in Russia as a foreign national

Start with the commercial model, not the registration form. Company ownership, management, the right to work, immigration status and taxation are different legal questions.

Legal due diligence before investing in Russia

A persuasive presentation is not proof of ownership, profitability or the investor's rights. Establish the counterparty, the asset, existing liabilities and your exit mechanism before signing or transferring money.

Temporary residence under Decree No. 702

Eligible foreign nationals can apply for a temporary residence permit (RVP) outside the quota and without the usual language, history and law certificate. Eligibility and general refusal grounds still need to be checked. The decree does not guarantee a permit.

Visa expiry while an RVP or VNZh application is pending

Acceptance of certain RVP documents or a VNZh application can affect the obligation to leave Russia under Article 5(2). Do not confuse acceptance, extension of permitted stay, the visa document and migration registration.

Annual residence notification: the 2027 income changes

RVP and VNZh holders normally file a residence notification within two months after each completed year of residence calculated from receipt of the permit. The rules differ; new income-related provisions take effect on 1 January 2027.

Permanent residence with a Russian honours degree

A qualifying honours diploma from a full-time, state-accredited higher education programme in Russia can provide a direct route to VNZh without an earlier RVP. A diploma alone does not resolve lawful stay or every general requirement.

Dismissal of a highly qualified specialist (HQS)

Early termination of an HQS employment or civil contract can trigger a 30-working-day statutory period to find another qualifying employer and obtain a new permit under the prescribed procedure. A separate 30-calendar-day departure period may apply if no new contract is concluded or the petition is rejected and no other lawful basis exists.

Migration registration of a foreign tenant

Migration registration is tied to the actual place of stay, not a purchased address. The receiving party usually notifies the authorities, but the law also provides routes for a foreign tenant to act personally in specified circumstances.

Read guide →Business

Can a foreign national register as an individual entrepreneur?

Foreign citizens may register as individual entrepreneurs in Russia if the statutory requirements are met, including documentation of the right to temporary or permanent residence. A visitor visa or migration card alone is not equivalent to the required residence document.

USN or AUSN: choosing a tax regime in Russia

Choosing between the simplified system (USN) and automated simplified system (AUSN) requires more than comparing rates. Review eligibility, revenue, staff, expense structure, VAT and banking.

Read guide →Tax

Foreign income and overseas accounts when living in Russia

Tax residence, currency-law residence and migration status are different concepts. A Russian RVP or VNZh is not enough by itself to determine tax treatment of every receipt from abroad.

Online cash registers and payment receipts for an IP

Cash-register duties depend on the seller, customer, transaction and payment method — not on citizenship or whether the payment reaches a bank account. Online payment does not automatically remove the receipt obligation.
