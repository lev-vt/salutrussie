Renting a home in Russia

We help foreign nationals and families find a suitable apartment or house, agree terms with the owner, prepare a rental agreement and address migration registration requirements.

Finding a home in a new country is not always easy

Where should you live? How do you negotiate with a landlord if you do not speak Russian yet? How can you check that the documents are in order and avoid registration problems after moving in?

We understand the difficulties foreign nationals face when looking for housing in Russia. We help with practical as well as legal matters so you can settle into your new home with greater confidence.

How we can help

Finding a home

We help arrange a search for an apartment or house through our partners, taking into account your budget, neighbourhood, transport, workplace, schools and nurseries.

Negotiating with the owner

We discuss living arrangements, occupants, pets, rental duration and whether the necessary immigration documents can be arranged.

Checking the owner and documents

We verify rights to the property, the signatory's authority and circumstances that could prevent you from living there lawfully.

Preparing and reviewing the rental agreement

We negotiate payment terms, the security deposit, rental period, termination provisions and the parties' responsibilities. We also help with translation.

Payments and handover

We check who should receive the money and on what legal basis. We help document payments, the handover and the condition of the property when you move in.

Migration registration

We identify the applicable procedure based on your immigration status and place of residence, and help arrange the necessary documents with the owner.

IMPORTANT FOR FOREIGN NATIONALS

Renting a home and migration registration

When choosing a home, discuss migration registration at your place of stay or registration at your place of residence with the owner in advance, depending on which procedure applies to you. Signing a rental agreement does not replace the required immigration formalities.

We explain the procedure to the owner, review documents and help both parties comply with the applicable requirements. The specific procedure depends on your nationality, immigration status and region of residence.

We only assist with lawful registration at the address where you actually live, in accordance with the applicable rules.

Frequently asked questions

Can I start looking for housing before arriving in Russia?

Yes. We discuss your budget, city, neighbourhood, family size and other requirements. We can arrange a preliminary search and review listings through our partners. We advise against transferring money for a property that has not been checked.

Can a foreign national rent an apartment in Russia?

Yes. It is important to check the documents, agree living arrangements with the owner and separately establish the applicable migration registration procedure.

Can the rental agreement be bilingual?

We can prepare or review the agreement and arrange translation so you understand the terms you are signing. For a bilingual agreement, we establish which language version takes precedence.

What if the landlord refuses to arrange migration registration?

It is best to clarify this before signing the agreement. We check which procedure applies to you, explain the owner's obligations and help identify a lawful solution.

Can you help with an apartment I found myself?

Yes. We can review a specific property and rental agreement, negotiate with the owner or work alongside your estate agent.

Can you help with a dispute over the return of a security deposit?

Yes. We examine the agreement, payment and handover documents, assess the claims and help resolve the dispute, including representing you in court if necessary.

Tell us where and when you plan to live, what kind of home you need and your budget. We will help arrange the search and provide legal support with renting.
