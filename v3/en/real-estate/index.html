Property rules for foreign nationals

Foreign nationals can rent homes and acquire many types of property in Russia. However, special restrictions apply, particularly to certain categories of land and designated territories. Some transactions and payments are also subject to special procedures.

We check which rules apply to you before you make a financial commitment. Buying property does not in itself give a foreign national the right to reside permanently in Russia.

Protecting your legal interests

Property law is one of our areas of legal practice. We can work with your estate agent, seller or landlord, or arrange a property search through our partners. As your lawyers, our role is to protect your interests.
