SPECIAL CONSIDERATIONS FOR FOREIGN BUYERS

Can foreign nationals buy property in Russia?

Yes. As a general rule, foreign nationals can buy apartments, houses and commercial premises. Special restrictions apply to land and certain transactions.

  • Foreign nationals cannot own land in designated border areas and certain other specially designated territories.
  • As a general rule, foreign nationals, stateless persons, foreign legal entities and companies in which the combined share held by foreign nationals, foreign legal entities and stateless persons exceeds 50% may only lease land governed by Federal Law No. 101-FZ. The law excludes certain types of plots, including garden plots, vegetable garden plots and land intended for personal subsidiary farming. Other applicable restrictions must also be checked (Articles 1 and 3 of Federal Law No. 101-FZ).
  • Certain transactions involving foreign persons are subject to special rules on completing transactions, obtaining approvals and making payments.

Before you make an advance payment or pay a deposit, we assess your legal status as a buyer, the property and the transaction terms. If you cannot lawfully acquire the property, we help you explore permitted alternatives.

Legal basis: Article 15 of the Russian Land Code, Article 3 of Federal Law No. 101-FZ; Bank of Russia guidance on special economic measures.

Full legal support throughout your purchase

Finding a property

We help define your property requirements, preferred region and budget. We arrange a search with our partners or work with a property you have already selected.

Legal due diligence

We examine the seller's rights, the property's ownership history, restrictions, encumbrances and other risks. Learn more about legal due diligence →

Negotiating with the seller

We represent your interests when negotiating the price, transaction terms, deadlines and the parties' obligations.

Preparing contracts

We prepare and review preliminary agreements, advance payment or earnest money agreements and sale contracts. We help arrange translation and notarisation where required.

Arranging payments

We check applicable banking and currency regulations and agree lawful payment arrangements, including the conditions under which the seller receives the funds.

Registering ownership

We assist with submitting documents to Rosreestr, Russia's property registration authority, to register the transfer of ownership, and with handing the property over to the buyer.

Your interests come first

An independent legal assessment is important when buying property. We represent your interests, not the seller's. We can work with your estate agent or developer, or arrange a property search through our partners.

If we identify significant risks, we explain them before you sign and recommend ways to address them. The decision to proceed is yours.

Buying property remotely

If you are still abroad, we can start with a consultation, property search and document review. Where necessary, we arrange legal representation under a power of attorney, check how it must be executed and agree which steps require your personal involvement.

Property and moving to Russia

It is best to plan your property purchase and immigration strategy together. Buying property does not in itself grant the right to reside in Russia. We help identify a separate legal basis for moving and assess special investment-based routes where applicable.

Frequently asked questions

Can a foreign national buy an apartment in Russia without a residence permit?

Generally, yes. Neither a temporary residence permit (RVP) nor a permanent residence permit (VNZh) is a prerequisite for buying an apartment. However, you need to check the restrictions, payment procedures and documentation applicable to your particular transaction.

Can I buy a house with land?

Yes, if you are legally entitled to acquire the land concerned. We check the land category, location and applicable restrictions before you pay a deposit.

Can I buy agricultural land?

As a general rule, foreign nationals may only lease land governed by the law on agricultural land transactions. We assess whether a specific plot can be acquired separately.

Can I buy property while outside Russia?

In some cases, a transaction can be arranged through a representative under a power of attorney. We check requirements for the power of attorney, its authentication, translation, payments and ownership registration.

Does buying property entitle me to permanent residence?

Buying property does not automatically entitle you to temporary or permanent residence. If you have other legal grounds, including specific investment-based grounds, eligibility is assessed separately.

Can I contact you if I have already found a property?

Yes. We can assist at any stage, conduct independent legal due diligence or provide full transaction support.

Tell us about your plans, preferred region, property type and budget. If you have already found a property, we can begin with legal due diligence.
