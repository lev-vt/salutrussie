INVESTING IN RUSSIA

Finding investment opportunities in Russia

We help foreign investors find projects, assets and business partners in Russia. We arrange a tailored search based on your investment goals, budget and preferences.

Discuss your investment search →

Types of projects we can help you find

Existing businesses and company interests

Businesses available for acquisition, equity participation or joint business development.

Commercial property

Offices, retail premises, warehouses and other properties suited to your goals.

Manufacturing and agriculture

Businesses, manufacturing sites and agricultural projects, taking into account permitted forms of foreign investment.

Technology projects and startups

Technology opportunities, including strategic partnerships and new product development.

The search is not limited to these categories. Our ability to identify a particular project depends on your criteria and the opportunities available.

We start with your investment goals

Are you looking to acquire an existing business, take part in managing a company, establish a joint venture or find an asset to hold for the long term? We tailor the search to each investor.

We consider your budget, preferred regions and industries, investment experience, desired level of involvement, timescale and risk tolerance. If you are investing as part of a move to Russia, we also take your immigration goals into account when defining the search criteria.

How we organise the search

  1. Define the search criteria. We agree your goals, budget, sector, region and preferred investment structure.
  2. Identify suitable opportunities. We organise the search and communicate with owners, potential partners and specialist advisers.
  3. Conduct preliminary screening. We examine available project information, request relevant documents and identify matters requiring further investigation.
  4. Present options to the investor. We help compare opportunities against the agreed criteria and arrange meetings and negotiations.
  5. Proceed to legal due diligence. Once a project is selected, we assess legal risks and develop transaction terms within the agreed scope of our services.

From finding a project to protecting your investment

Our role goes beyond identifying opportunities. As a legal team, we consider asset ownership, ownership structures, participation terms and potential risks from the preliminary screening stage.

Before committing funds, we recommend independent legal due diligence and, where necessary, financial and technical assessments by specialist advisers. The final investment decision remains yours.
