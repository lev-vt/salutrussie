INVESTING IN RUSSIA

Investment project legal support

We represent foreign investors throughout the implementation of investment projects in Russia. We assist with negotiations, transactions, financing, corporate governance and ongoing project operations.

STRATEGY FIRST, IMPLEMENTATION SECOND

Your legal representative throughout the investment project

Signing an investment agreement is only the beginning. It is equally important that commitments are fulfilled, that you receive the agreed rights and information about the project, and that issues are addressed promptly.

We can become your ongoing legal partner in Russia, represent your interests in dealings with other project participants and coordinate legal work with specialist advisers.

How we can help

Negotiating with partners

We represent your interests and negotiate commercial and legal terms of participation, allocation of responsibilities and decision-making mechanisms.

Preparing and completing transactions

We draft investment and corporate agreements, agreements between company members, asset acquisition documents and other required documents.

Legal support for financing

We agree payment terms, financing stages, evidence of contractual performance and contractual safeguards for your capital.

Corporate governance

We assist with establishing and developing project companies, corporate decisions, exercise of investor rights and changes to the project structure.

Monitoring contractual performance

We monitor agreed legal milestones, review documents and compliance with contractual obligations, and help you respond promptly to breaches.

Dispute resolution and project exit

We assist with resolving conflicts, amending or terminating contractual relationships, selling company interests and other exit arrangements. Where necessary, we protect your interests in court.

Managing investments from abroad

We can represent you in Russia within the agreed scope of authority: participate in negotiations, communicate with Russian partners, review legal documents and keep you informed about the project's progress.

Where necessary, we assist with powers of attorney and coordinate with notaries, banks, government authorities and specialist advisers. The scope of representation is agreed individually.

From individual transactions to ongoing legal support

We can assist at any stage: when preparing a transaction, during project implementation or when difficulties arise with partners. We can handle a particular stage or take responsibility for an agreed scope of ongoing legal work.

Before we start, we agree the tasks, authority, communication and reporting arrangements. Investment and management decisions remain with the investor.
