IN LEGAL PRACTICE SINCE 2005

Investing is only part of the process. Protecting your investment matters too.

Before making a financial commitment, you need to understand what rights you are acquiring, who owns the assets, who controls the project and how you can exit.

Drawing on our experience in commercial and corporate disputes, we assess legal risks and help structure investment terms, control mechanisms, investor safeguards and repayment arrangements where repayment is provided for by contract.

Our role is to provide an independent legal assessment and protect your interests. Where necessary, we arrange financial and technical assessments with specialist advisers. Legal due diligence does not guarantee investment returns.

Legal support at every stage

  1. Investment strategy. We establish your goals, project criteria and preferred form of participation.
  2. Search and due diligence. We identify opportunities and conduct a legal review of the selected project.
  3. Transaction preparation. We agree the structure, contracts, financing terms and safeguards.
  4. Project implementation. We assist with contractual performance, corporate decisions and the protection of your interests as an investor.

Investment and moving to Russia

If you plan to move to Russia, we can assess eligibility for a permanent residence permit (VNZh) without first obtaining a temporary residence permit (RVP), for investors and qualifying family members under Article 8(2)(17) of Federal Law No. 115-FZ.

Government Resolution No. 2573 of 31 December 2022 provides four routes: at least RUB 15 million invested in socially significant regional projects over the preceding three years; establishing and operating your own company with at least RUB 4 million in annual taxes, levies and contributions; investing at least RUB 30 million in an established Russian company (subject to additional operating history and payment requirements); or acquiring qualifying property with a cadastral value of at least RUB 50 million in Moscow, RUB 20 million in the Far East or RUB 25 million elsewhere in Russia.

Each route has additional conditions, including relevant holding periods, documentation, ownership structure and absence of encumbrances where required. Not every investment qualifies for residence. We assess eligibility and applicable restrictions before you select a project or transfer funds.
