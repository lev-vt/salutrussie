Investment due diligence and legal support

Before investing, understand exactly what you are acquiring

An attractive project presentation does not always reflect its actual legal position. Assets may belong to another company, important rights may be restricted and liabilities to third parties may not have been disclosed to the investor.

We review documents and transaction circumstances as independent lawyers acting in your interests. Our experience in commercial, corporate and insolvency disputes helps us assess not only the validity of documents but also the potential consequences of conflicts.

Our role is to identify legal risks before you commit your money.

What our legal due diligence covers

Company and ownership structure

We review company members, beneficial owners to the extent information is available, corporate documents, management authority and the structure of the corporate group.

Rights to assets

We examine ownership of property, equipment, company interests, intellectual property and other material assets, including the basis of ownership and encumbrances.

Contracts and liabilities

We analyse key commercial contracts, credit facilities, loans, guarantees, sureties, restrictions and change-of-control provisions.

Litigation and insolvency risks

We review available information about court cases, enforcement proceedings, insolvency and circumstances that could lead to a transaction being challenged.

Partners and corporate risks

We assess authority, relationships between company members, decision-making procedures, conflicts of interest and mechanisms for protecting investor rights.

Restrictions on foreign investors

We check applicable foreign investment requirements, special approval procedures and restrictions relating to sectors, assets and payments. This includes assessing whether prior approval is required under Federal Law No. 57-FZ for strategic companies or authorisation is needed for certain transactions involving LLC interests and management rights under Presidential Decree No. 618 (Bank of Russia guidance, in Russian). We consider ownership structures, indirect control, the parties' status and applicable sanctions restrictions.

Tax and financial regulatory risks

We analyse the legal aspects of liabilities, payments and tax risks. Where necessary, we arrange involvement of tax and financial specialists.

Investment and exit terms

We examine the rights you will acquire, how you will exercise control, obtain information, dispose of your interest and exit the project.

We tailor the scope of due diligence to the transaction structure, industry, documents available and size of the investment.

What you receive after due diligence

  • A legal opinion describing material findings and identified risks.
  • An assessment of potential consequences for the investor, including litigation risks.
  • Recommendations on mitigating risks, obtaining additional documents and changing transaction terms.
  • A legal assessment of whether to proceed and recommendations on investment structuring.

If we identify significant risks that cannot be adequately mitigated, we tell the investor directly. A legal opinion does not guarantee investment returns or the absence of future disputes.

From legal due diligence to transaction structuring

After reviewing the project, we help develop a legal structure for the investment, negotiate terms with partners and prepare the required documents.

  • Identify the form of participation: acquisition of an asset or company interest, a joint venture, project financing or another permitted arrangement.
  • Agree financing terms, staged payments and the transfer of rights.
  • Develop mechanisms for corporate control, reporting and investor protection.
  • Provide for contractual safeguards, the parties' liability, dispute resolution and exit arrangements.

Frequently asked questions

Can I request due diligence on a project I found myself?

Yes. We provide independent legal due diligence regardless of who introduced the investment opportunity.

Do you assess the financial model and expected returns?

Our core expertise is legal due diligence. Where necessary, we arrange involvement of financial, tax and technical specialists for their respective assessments.

What if due diligence reveals significant risks?

We recommend ways to eliminate or reduce risks, renegotiate transaction terms or advise against proceeding if the risks are unacceptable.

Can you assist with the transaction after due diligence?

Yes. We structure the transaction, prepare contracts, assist with negotiations and support the agreed stages of implementation.

Considering an investment project in Russia?

Contact us before signing binding commitments or transferring funds. We will agree the scope of due diligence and recommend a strategy for protecting your interests.
