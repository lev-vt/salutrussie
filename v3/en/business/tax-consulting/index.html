DOING BUSINESS IN RUSSIA

Tax advice

We help foreign nationals, entrepreneurs and companies understand the Russian tax system, choose a lawful and commercially sound way of operating and reduce tax risks.

Discuss your tax questions →

Plan your tax strategy in advance

Your business structure, tax regime and contractual terms affect your costs and financial results. Tax implications are especially important if you are moving to Russia, continue to receive income from abroad or operate an international business.

We help align legal and tax considerations before you register a business, enter into a transaction or restructure your operations.

How we can help

Choosing a tax regime

We compare tax regimes available to sole traders and LLCs, including the general tax system (OSNO), simplified tax system (USN), automated simplified tax system (AUSN), patent-based tax system and professional income tax, where the relevant eligibility requirements are met.

Personal tax residence

We help determine your tax residence, explain how it differs from immigration status and assess how moving to Russia affects the taxation of your income.

Foreign income and international taxation

We examine foreign income, applicable international tax agreements, dividend taxation and obligations relating to overseas accounts and companies.

Tax implications of transactions

We assess tax and currency regulation issues relating to contracts, international payments, property purchases and sales, and interests in companies.

Working with your accountant

We help align legal decisions with tax and accounting requirements. Where necessary, we coordinate with specialist advisers.

Tax risks and disputes

We help assess demands from tax authorities, prepare legal arguments, objections and appeals, and represent you in court where necessary.

Tax considerations for foreign nationals in Russia

Nationality, immigration status, tax residence and residence status under currency regulations are distinct legal concepts. Obtaining a temporary residence permit (RVP) or permanent residence permit (VNZh) does not automatically resolve all tax matters.

If you are moving to Russia, receive income in several countries or own a foreign business, establish your obligations in each relevant jurisdiction in advance. We consider the applicable rules and your particular circumstances.

For questions of foreign law, we involve advisers from the relevant jurisdiction where necessary.
