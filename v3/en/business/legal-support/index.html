Business legal support

We protect the interests of entrepreneurs, business owners and companies in Russia. We help you make commercial decisions, manage business relationships, complete transactions and resolve complex legal disputes.

IN LEGAL PRACTICE SINCE 2005

Your business goals shape our legal strategy

We work directly with business owners, executives and in-house legal teams. We understand that you need more than a legally correct document. You need a solution that helps your business grow, protects its assets and manages risk.

Our team, led by Lev Vtiurin, combines commercial and corporate legal experience with hands-on experience in complex litigation. We assess legal solutions based on how they will work in practice, including if a dispute arises.

Strategy first, implementation second.

Our main areas of business legal support

Contracts and commercial transactions

We draft and review contracts, support negotiations, and structure payment terms, performance obligations and safeguards for your business.

Corporate law

We advise on corporate governance, relationships between members, transactions involving ownership interests, changes to company structures and corporate agreements.

Protecting business owners' interests

We help protect assets, ownership rights and control of the business. We assist with corporate conflicts and partners' exits from the business.

International business

We support projects involving foreign participants, international trade contracts and dealings with Russian and foreign counterparties. We assess applicable regulatory and payment restrictions.

Technology businesses and intellectual property

We advise technology companies, online businesses and startups. We work with software rights, licensing, technology, trade secrets and contractual structures for digital projects.

Employment law and immigration

We help formalise employment relationships, organise HR processes and comply with requirements for employing foreign workers. Learn more about immigration support for employers →

Commercial and corporate disputes

We handle negotiations, pre-litigation claims and court proceedings. We represent companies before courts of first instance, appeal and cassation and, where procedurally available, the Supreme Court of the Russian Federation.

Debt recovery and insolvency

We assist with debt recovery, disputes involving creditors and counterparties, insolvency proceedings and protecting the interests of business owners and executives.

A legal partner for international companies and entrepreneurs

If you are growing a business in Russia or managing a Russian company from abroad, we can handle the Russian legal aspects of your projects: communicate with partners, review documents, participate in negotiations and protect your interests within the agreed authority.

Where necessary, we coordinate our work with your overseas legal advisers, accountants and other specialists.

We work with individual entrepreneurs, small businesses and more complex corporate structures.

Frequently asked questions

Can you help with a single transaction or contract?

Yes. We assist with individual matters and projects and also offer ongoing legal support.

Can you work with our in-house legal team?

Yes. We can act as an external legal partner, handle the Russian aspects of an international project or take responsibility for a particular workstream.

Do you work with foreign business owners?

Yes. We advise on management, relationships between owners, transactions and company operations in Russia, taking foreign participation into account.

Do you represent companies in Russian courts?

Yes. We handle commercial and corporate disputes in Russia's state commercial courts and courts of general jurisdiction, including appeals to higher courts.

Can we work together remotely?

Yes. Consultations, document preparation and much of our ongoing legal support can be arranged remotely. We agree arrangements for representation separately.
