STRATEGY FIRST, IMPLEMENTATION SECOND

Setting up a company is only the first step

Before registering a business, it is important to understand how it will operate: who will own and manage it, how payments will be arranged, what authorisations are required and how the owners' interests will be protected.

For foreign entrepreneurs, it is particularly important to align the business plan with an immigration strategy. Owning a company, working in Russia and residing in the country are three separate legal matters.

We help you design a legal structure that supports your business goals, then put it into practice.
