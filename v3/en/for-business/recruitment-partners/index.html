INTERNATIONAL PARTNERSHIPS

For international recruitment companies and agencies

We welcome partnerships with companies recruiting internationally for jobs in Russia. You source the candidates; we handle the legal and organisational aspects of the project in Russia.

Your legal partner in Russia

  • Developing a lawful employment route for foreign workers.
  • Liaising with Russian employers and coordinating immigration procedures.
  • Preparing invitations, work visas and the necessary permits.
  • Post-arrival support, migration registration and notifications to the Ministry of Internal Affairs (MVD).
  • Legal support and protection of the interests of the parties involved.

We agree responsibilities between the agency, employer and our team, taking into account Russian law and the international recruitment rules in the country where candidates are recruited.
