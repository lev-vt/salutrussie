Legal support for business in Russia

International recruitment, work visas and permits, employment and commercial law. For Russian employers, overseas companies and recruitment partners.

We work with owners, managers, HR and in-house legal teams. Before promising a start date or committing to a project, we identify the actual employer, location of work, permits, applicable exceptions and critical deadlines.

How we can help

  • Immigration strategy for individual hires and groups
  • Employer permissions, invitations, work visas and work permits
  • MVD notifications, employment documentation and migration registration
  • Review of contracts, counterparties, authority and regulatory constraints
  • Compliance audits and corrective action plans
  • External legal counsel for recurring business needs

Project workflow

  1. Brief: countries, region, vacancies, headcount and timing
  2. Legal structure: worker status, employer and contract
  3. Implementation plan: documents, responsibilities and deadlines
  4. Agreed support and monitoring of changes

What to send us

For an initial review, describe your industry, countries of citizenship, number of employees, work location, planned dates and contractual model. Do not send candidate passports in the first message.
