International companies

Russian legal advice on staff mobility, work permits and visas, local business structures and contracts with Russian counterparties.

Sending specialists to Russia

Distinguish a business visit, employment and provision of services. A business visa is not a general work authorization. We check each specialist’s status, contract, applicable exceptions, host and actual activities.

Establishing a presence in Russia

Compare a Russian LLC, representative office, branch or contractual model. Assess permitted structures, signatory powers, tax and currency implications, regulatory restrictions and employment obligations.

Working with a Russian employer or partner

Allocate the foreign company's, employer's and intermediary's responsibilities. Review contracts, permissions, MVD filings and post-arrival compliance. We do not arrange sham invitations or visas.

Project deliverables

A legal issue and risk map, document list and implementation plan with responsibilities and deadlines. Execution is agreed separately. Government decisions are made by the competent authorities.
