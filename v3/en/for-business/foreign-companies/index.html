International companies

Doing business in Russia: business travel and work visas, lawful staff mobility, project structuring and contracts with Russian counterparties.

Sending specialists to Russia

We distinguish business travel from employment and the provision of services. A business visa is not a general authorisation to work. We assess each specialist's status, contractual arrangements, applicable exemptions, host organisation and actual activities.

Establishing a presence in Russia

We compare a Russian limited liability company (LLC), representative office, branch and contractual arrangements. We assess whether the proposed structure is permitted, the parties' authority, tax and currency implications, applicable restrictions and employment obligations.

Hiring through a Russian partner

We clarify the responsibilities of the foreign company, Russian employer and intermediaries. We review contracts, permits, responsibility for notifications to the Ministry of Internal Affairs (MVD) and formalities after arrival. We do not arrange fictitious invitations or visas.

What you receive

A written assessment of legal issues and risks, a document checklist and an action plan setting out responsibilities and deadlines. Implementation is subject to a separately agreed engagement. Visa and permit decisions are made by the competent authorities.
