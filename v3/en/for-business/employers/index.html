For Russian employers

We provide legal support for your company's employment-related immigration needs, from planning the recruitment of foreign staff to their ongoing employment in Russia.

Comprehensive immigration support

  • Work visas, invitations and the necessary permits.
  • Migration registration, notifications to the Ministry of Internal Affairs (MVD) and review of employment documents.
  • Immigration compliance audits, deadline monitoring and prevention of violations.
  • Representation before government authorities and courts if disputes arise.

We assist with individual projects and offer ongoing legal support for your business.

International recruitment

If your company needs employees from abroad, we can help organise international recruitment with our overseas recruitment partners.

We clarify the employer's needs, coordinate with recruitment agencies and provide legal support for candidates' entry and lawful employment in Russia.
