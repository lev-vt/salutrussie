IN LEGAL PRACTICE SINCE 2005

A document should protect your interests, not just meet formal requirements

We do more than fill in standard templates. First, we establish the outcome you need, the legal consequences of the document and the risks that must be addressed.

Our litigation experience helps us anticipate how contractual terms and legal wording may be interpreted if a dispute arises.

Documents for foreign nationals and companies

If a document is executed outside Russia or intended for use in another country, we check requirements for its form, signatory authority, notarisation, translation, apostille or consular legalisation in advance.

Where necessary, we prepare bilingual documents and coordinate with notaries and translators. We involve specialist advisers on foreign law where appropriate.

How we work

  1. Define the task. We establish your goals, circumstances and the purpose of the document.
  2. Review the source materials. We check documents, factual information, authority and legal risks.
  3. Draft or review the document. We prepare a tailored document or recommend amendments to your existing draft.
  4. Agree the final version. We explain the key terms, signing procedure and next steps.

Most documents can be prepared remotely. Where necessary, we separately agree assistance with signing, submitting documents or representation.
