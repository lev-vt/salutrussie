More than immigration law

SalutRussie is a project of a team of lawyers led by Lev Vtiurin. Our practice covers civil, commercial, corporate and immigration law, litigation and legal support for transactions.

Our litigation experience helps us assess legal solutions in light of their practical consequences. We aim not only to resolve your immediate issue but also to prevent potential problems in the future.

How we work

We start with a legal consultation: identify your goals, examine the circumstances and documents, and assess possible courses of action and risks.

We propose a strategy, agree the scope of work and put it into practice. Where necessary, we can represent your interests in Russia even if you are abroad. We agree the form of representation and the necessary authority on a case-by-case basis.
