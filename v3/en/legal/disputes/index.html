Effective litigation starts with strategy

Litigation is one of the core areas of our team's practice under the leadership of Lev Vtiurin. We handle complex civil, commercial, corporate and administrative disputes.

Before going to court, we assess the merits of the case, the evidence, the other party's possible actions and the practical value of a future judgment. Obtaining a favourable judgment matters, but so does understanding how it can be enforced.

Strategy first, implementation second.

Representation at every stage of litigation

We handle cases before Russia's state commercial courts and courts of general jurisdiction. We can assist before proceedings begin or at later stages.

  • Pre-litigation settlement and formal claims.
  • Proceedings before courts of first instance.
  • Appeal and cassation proceedings.
  • Applications to the Supreme Court of the Russian Federation where procedurally available.
  • Assistance with enforcement of court judgments.

At each stage, we identify available legal remedies based on the circumstances and procedural deadlines.

How we work

  1. Assess your situation. We review documents, evidence, deadlines and the parties' legal positions.
  2. Develop a strategy. We assess prospects, risks, settlement options and litigation remedies.
  3. Put the agreed plan into action. We prepare documents, participate in negotiations and court hearings, and file the necessary motions.
  4. Assess the next steps. Where necessary, we assist with appeals and enforcement of the judgment.

We do not promise a particular court outcome in advance. We explain realistic prospects and recommend a legally sound strategy for protecting your interests.

If you are outside Russia

We can arrange remote consultations and document preparation, and represent your interests in Russia under a power of attorney where permitted by law. We help identify the requirements for executing powers of attorney, translating and authenticating foreign documents.

We separately assess whether your personal participation is required and agree how we will communicate throughout the case.

Frequently asked questions

Can you represent me if I am outside Russia?

Yes, in matters where representation is permitted. We establish the necessary authority and power of attorney requirements, and check whether you must participate personally.

Can I contact you if my case is already before a court?

Yes. We review the case materials, procedural deadlines and legal position already taken, and assess how we can join the case and what steps are available.

Can an existing judgment be challenged?

It depends on the type of case, stage of proceedings, date of the judgment and grounds for appeal. We assess the available procedural options individually.

Do I have to go to court?

No. If your interests can be protected through negotiation, a formal claim or a settlement agreement, we consider that option alongside litigation.

Need to protect your interests in Russia?

Tell us about your situation. If you have received a judgment, a statement of claim or a formal demand, keep the documents and note the date you received them. The earlier we become involved, the more options we may have to protect your rights.
