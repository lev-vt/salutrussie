Comprehensive legal consultation

Tailored legal strategy

For complex situations and long-term projects, we develop a step-by-step action plan based on your goals, documents and circumstances.

Consultations are held remotely or in another agreed format. Where needed, we prepare a written legal opinion or action plan, with the contents agreed in advance.

How it works

  1. Get in touch. Briefly describe your situation and the outcome you want.
  2. Agree the consultation format. We identify the assistance you need, confirm the fee and agree a consultation time.
  3. Have your consultation. We discuss your questions and documents within the agreed scope and explain the options and risks.
  4. Identify the next steps. If further legal support is needed, we agree the tasks and terms of cooperation separately.

Please do not send passport details or other sensitive documents in your first message. If needed, we will agree a secure way to share them.
