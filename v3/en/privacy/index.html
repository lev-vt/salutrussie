Personal data processing policy

How personal data is processed when you request legal assistance.

Draft for approval. Not yet in force. Payments are not taken directly on this website.

1. Data controller and scope

This policy sets out how Innovative Legal Solutions LLC processes personal data when legal services are requested through SalutRussie. Data subjects include website visitors, prospective and existing clients, representatives of organisations and individuals whose information has been lawfully supplied for the provision of services. Contact details for data protection enquiries are provided below.

2. Purposes, data and legal bases

Personal data is processed for specific lawful purposes and limited to what is necessary. Where consent is the legal basis, it is obtained separately from the agreement and other documents confirmed by the data subject, in accordance with Article 9 of Federal Law No. 152-FZ. Visiting the website alone does not constitute consent to all processing.

Please do not send your passport, medical documents or criminal record information in your first enquiry. The website is not intended to collect special categories of personal data or biometric personal data. Where health information, criminal record information or other data subject to special legal rules is required for a particular case, the permissibility and legal basis of processing and the secure transmission method must first be assessed. Information about minors is supplied by a legal representative or on another basis permitted by law.

3. How data is processed

Processing may include collection, recording, organisation, accumulation, storage, updating, retrieval, use, disclosure in permitted cases, blocking, deletion and destruction. Both automated and non-automated processing may be used. Processing is limited to the relevant purposes; decisions producing legal effects are not taken solely by automated means without a basis provided by law.

4. Communication channels, third parties and cross-border transfers

The website contains links to email, Telegram and WhatsApp. Clicking a link does not send your message automatically; when an external service is opened, its provider may receive technical connection information. Information you send through the chosen service may be processed under that service's own terms. Using external communication channels does not relieve the controller of its data protection obligations. We will agree a secure channel for sharing documents. Recipients may include authorised staff, engaged specialists where a lawful basis exists, government authorities as required by law, and infrastructure providers subject to applicable requirements for outsourced processing.

Before processing begins on the live website, the hosting, email, analytics, CRM and payment providers, database locations and any required notifications to Roskomnadzor must be confirmed. When collecting Russian citizens' personal data, including online, recording, organisation, accumulation, storage, updating and retrieval using databases outside Russia are prohibited except for the exceptions expressly provided in Article 18(5) of Federal Law No. 152-FZ. Cross-border transfers must comply with Article 12 and applicable restrictions, including separate advance notification where required. The use of a foreign messaging service does not remove these obligations.

5. Cookies, analytics and payments

In the current version, the consultations and contact pages direct visitors to external communication channels. Article search takes place in the browser without sending the query to a separate search service. The website does not accept bank card details. The site's shared code contains conditional analytics calls that run only when an analytics counter is connected. The actual use or absence of optional cookies, external analytics counters and technical logs must be verified before launch. Before enabling optional analytics or advertising, the applicable legal regime will be determined and separate consent obtained where necessary. Payment methods are agreed individually. If an external payment service is used, information about the payment provider and applicable terms is communicated before payment. Do not provide the controller with passwords, verification codes or full card details.

6. Retention and termination of processing

Data is retained no longer than necessary for the relevant purpose, or for a mandatory retention period under law or contract. Once the purpose has been achieved, consent withdrawn or the legal basis ceases to apply, data is deleted or destroyed within the periods and according to the procedures prescribed by Article 21 of Federal Law No. 152-FZ, unless another lawful basis exists. For enquiries that do not result in an agreement, retention is determined by the need to respond and conclude negotiations; for client matters, by performance of the agreement and lawful grounds for continued retention, including protection of rights; and for payment and accounting documents, by statutory retention periods. Specific retention periods by category and procedures for confirming destruction must be approved before this policy enters into force. Paper and electronic media are destroyed in a manner that prevents further use of the destroyed information.

7. Data subject rights

You may request information about processing, require correction, blocking or destruction of data in cases prescribed by law, withdraw consent, request termination of processing and lodge a complaint with Roskomnadzor or a court. Send your request using the contact details provided; only information necessary to verify your identity may be requested. Requests to terminate processing are handled within the period prescribed by Article 21 of Federal Law No. 152-FZ, generally within 10 working days, subject to a reasoned extension within statutory limits. Processing may continue if an independent lawful basis exists. Data access requests are handled under Articles 14 and 20 of Federal Law No. 152-FZ. Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of prior processing.

8. Security and updates

The controller must implement necessary legal, organisational and technical measures, including access controls, confidentiality, monitoring of processing, protection of storage media and incident response as prescribed by law. Measures actually implemented and the appointment of responsible persons must be confirmed before launch. The policy is updated when processing activities change; new purposes require an appropriate lawful basis. Draft dated 24 November 2026.

9. Processing data of international clients

The applicability of the GDPR depends, among other things, on whether services are offered to individuals located in the EU, rather than solely on their nationality or language (Article 3 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679). Where processing falls within the GDPR, the relevant data subject rights, transparency requirements and safeguards must be ensured. Before such processing begins, the legal bases, information provided to data subjects, recipients and cross-border transfer mechanism must be confirmed, together with an EU representative where required. GDPR / RGPD.
