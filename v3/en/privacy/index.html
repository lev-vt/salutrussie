Personal data and privacy policy

How personal data are handled when you contact us for legal support.

Review draft; not yet in force. Online payments are disabled in this preview.

1. Controller and scope

ООО «ИННОВАЦИОННЫЕ ПРАВОВЫЕ РЕШЕНИЯ» is the controller for legal-service inquiries handled under the SalutRussie brand. This policy covers visitors, prospective and existing clients, organizational representatives and persons whose information is lawfully supplied for a case.

2. Purposes, categories and lawful grounds

  • Inquiries and orders: name, contact details, language, message and necessary case information; steps requested by the data subject before contracting, or separate consent when required.
  • Legal services: necessary facts and documents, representative information and third-party data only on an appropriate legal basis; contract performance, legal duties or consent where required.
  • Billing, accounting and disputes: order details, payer information, payment and receipt records, correspondence; performance and statutory obligations or protection of legal rights.
  • Website operation: technical request information such as IP address, time and browser settings may be processed by hosting infrastructure. The production provider and retention schedule must be confirmed before launch.

Do not send passports, medical records or criminal-record information in an initial message. This preview does not collect such data through forms. Special-category or biometric data require a separate appropriate basis and secure procedure; children's information requires lawful representation or another statutory basis.

3. Processing and recipients

Processing includes collection, recording, organization, storage, updating, use, lawful disclosure, blocking, deletion and destruction, by automated or manual means. Data are limited to the necessary purpose. Recipients may include authorized staff, agreed specialists, lawful public authorities and contracted infrastructure providers. No solely automated decision with legal effects is made without a lawful basis.

4. External channels and cross-border transfers

Email, Telegram and WhatsApp links open external services; a message is sent only when you take that action. Those services may process data under their own terms. Confirm a suitable secure channel before sending documents. Production hosting, mail, CRM, analytics and payment processors, database locations and applicable notifications must be verified before launch. Russian citizens' data collection must comply with Article 18(5) of Federal Law No. 152-FZ; cross-border transfers must comply with Article 12, including prior notification when required.

5. Cookies and payment

This review version has no first-party inquiry form, advertising tracker, active payment gateway or card-data collection. Hosting may process technical data. Optional analytics and advertising must be assessed separately and consent obtained where required. Payment provider details and conditions will be disclosed before payment is enabled.

6. Retention and rights

Data are retained only while needed for the purpose or statutory or contractual retention requirements. On expiry or withdrawal of consent, data are deleted or destroyed under Article 21 unless another lawful basis remains. You may request access, correction, blocking, erasure or cessation of processing, withdraw consent and complain to Roskomnadzor or a court. Identity verification is limited to necessary information. Requests are handled within statutory periods; cessation requests generally within ten working days, subject to legal exceptions.

7. Security and updates

Appropriate legal, organizational and technical safeguards must include access controls, confidentiality, secure storage and incident handling. Actual production measures and responsible persons are to be confirmed before activation. This policy is a review draft dated 24 November 2026. If international privacy law applies to specific processing, its mandatory requirements are preserved.
