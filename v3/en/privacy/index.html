Personal data processing policy

How personal data is processed when you seek legal assistance.

Draft for approval. Not yet in force. Online payments are disabled in the preview.

1. Controller and scope

This policy defines the processing of personal data by ООО «ИННОВАЦИОННЫЕ ПРАВОВЫЕ РЕШЕНИЯ» when legal services are requested through SalutRussie. Data subjects include website visitors, prospective and existing clients, representatives of organisations and persons whose information is lawfully provided for the delivery of services. The data protection contact is given below.

2. Purposes, data and legal grounds

PurposeData categoriesLegal ground
Responding to inquiries and agreeing ordersName, contact details, inquiry text, preferred language, and information about the matter to the extent necessaryConclusion of a contract at the data subject's initiative; separate consent where necessary (Article 6 of Law No. 152-FZ)

Do not send passports, medical documents or criminal-record information in your initial inquiry. Special categories of data and biometric data are not collected via website forms; if processing is needed for a case, a separate lawful basis and secure channel must be established beforehand. A minor's data is provided by a legal representative or on another lawful basis.

3. How data is processed

Processing may include collection, recording, organisation, accumulation, storage, updating, retrieval, use, disclosure where permitted, blocking, deletion and destruction. Both automated and non-automated processing are used. Processing is limited to its purposes; decisions with legal consequences are not taken solely by automated means without a statutory basis.

4. Communication channels, third parties and cross-border transfers

The website contains links to email, Telegram and WhatsApp. Following a link does not send your message automatically; data in the selected service may be processed under its terms. We will propose an agreed secure channel for documents. Recipients may include authorised employees, engaged specialists where a lawful basis exists, public authorities as required by law, and infrastructure providers subject to requirements governing delegated processing.

Before processing begins on the live website, hosting, email, analytics, CRM and payment providers, database locations and any required notifications to Roskomnadzor must be confirmed. Article 18(5) of Law No. 152-FZ applies when collecting personal data of Russian citizens. Cross-border transfers must comply with Article 12 and applicable restrictions, including a separate prior notification where required. Using a foreign messaging service does not remove these obligations.

5. Cookies, analytics and payments

The preview does not use its own data collection forms, advertising trackers, payment gateway or card-data collection. The hosting provider may process technical information under its own rules. Before optional analytics or advertising is enabled, the applicable legal regime will be determined and separate consent obtained where required. Once enabled, payments are processed by a verified payment provider; its details and terms will be disclosed before payment.

6. Retention and termination of processing

Data is retained no longer than necessary for the relevant purpose, or for a mandatory retention period under law or contract. Once the purpose is achieved, consent is withdrawn or the legal basis ceases, data is deleted or destroyed within the time limits and procedure in Article 21 of Law No. 152-FZ, unless another lawful basis exists. Retention periods for document categories are approved in the controller's internal rules; accounting and tax records are retained for statutory periods.

7. Data subject rights

You may request information on processing, require correction, blocking or destruction of data where provided by law, withdraw consent, request termination of processing and complain to Roskomnadzor or a court. Send your request to the stated contact; only information necessary to verify identity may be required. A request to stop processing is considered within the period specified in Article 21, generally no more than ten working days; processing may continue where a lawful basis exists. Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of prior processing.

8. Security and updates

The controller must apply necessary legal, organisational and technical measures, including access controls, confidentiality, oversight of processing, protection of storage media and incident response according to the prescribed procedure. Measures actually implemented and the appointment of responsible persons must be confirmed before launch. The policy is updated when processes change; new purposes require an appropriate lawful basis. Draft dated 24 November 2026.

9. Processing data of international clients

For services directed at persons in the EU, applicability of the GDPR is assessed separately (Articles 3, 13–14, 27 and Chapter V of Regulation (EU) 2016/679). Before such processing begins, the legal grounds, information for data subjects, recipients and cross-border transfer mechanism must be confirmed, together with an EU representative where necessary. GDPR / RGPD.
