What is a temporary residence permit?

A temporary residence permit (RVP) gives a foreign national the legal right to reside in Russia. As a general rule, it is issued for three years.

With a temporary residence permit, you can:

Live in Russia for the duration of your permit
Work without a work patent or work permit
Access compulsory health insurance (OMS)
Study in Russia
Set up and grow a business
Apply later for a permanent residence permit (VNZh), if eligible

An RVP is tied to a specific Russian region. Statutory territorial restrictions and exceptions apply to where you can live and work.

STANDARD PROCEDURE

Applying under the quota system

As a general rule, temporary residence permits are issued within an annual quota allocated by the Russian government to each region.

Obtaining a quota place can feel like a lottery: places are limited and success is not guaranteed. A special commission nevertheless assesses each applicant's circumstances.

The commission considers employment in Russia, education, qualifications, relatives, property ownership and other circumstances specified by law. Preparing a strong application and supporting documents is therefore particularly important.

We assist with applications under the quota system.

We assess your prospects, prepare your quota application and supporting documents, and help you apply for a temporary residence permit once a quota place has been allocated.

Applying without a quota

Russian law provides several routes to a temporary residence permit without first obtaining a quota place.

Temporary residence under Decree 702

The Shared Values programme is designed for foreign nationals wishing to move to Russia. Eligible applicants can obtain an RVP without a quota or an examination in Russian language, history and law.

Learn more about Decree 702 →

Temporary residence through marriage to a Russian citizen

For foreign nationals who have been married for at least three years to a Russian citizen permanently residing in Russia, or who have a child together born or adopted during that marriage. The permit is issued in the region where the Russian spouse resides.

Temporary residence for children

For foreign nationals under 18 applying together with a foreign parent or whose foreign parent already holds a valid RVP.

Temporary residence for education

A special permit for foreign students enrolled full-time in qualifying programmes at state educational or research institutions. It is valid for the duration of the studies plus 180 calendar days.

Learn more about student support →

Temporary residence based on Russian education

For nationals of former Soviet republics who have completed qualifying vocational or professional education programmes in Russia at state educational or research institutions.

Temporary residence based on nationality

Special routes are available to nationals of Kazakhstan, Moldova and Ukraine.

Temporary residence through the compatriot resettlement programme

For participants in Russia's state programme for the voluntary resettlement of compatriots and eligible family members who hold the relevant status.

Other routes to temporary residence

Russian law provides additional special routes. We help you determine which ones apply to your circumstances.

Legal basis: Article 6 and Article 6.2 of Federal Law No. 115-FZ, and Presidential Decree No. 702.

You may qualify for permanent residence directly

Certain categories of foreign nationals can apply for a permanent residence permit (VNZh) without first obtaining temporary residence.

We therefore recommend developing your individual immigration strategy before starting the application process.

Frequently asked questions

How long is a temporary residence permit valid?

Generally, three years. Different validity periods apply to certain categories of foreign nationals.

Can I work in Russia with a temporary residence permit?

Yes, without a work patent or work permit, generally in the region where your RVP was issued.

Do I need a quota place?

No. If you qualify for a route to temporary residence that is exempt from the quota, you do not need to obtain one.

Can I apply for a temporary residence permit from abroad?

Yes. Russian law allows you to submit an application through a Russian consulate in your country of residence.

When can I apply for permanent residence after obtaining temporary residence?

As a general rule, you can apply after eight months of residence under an RVP, provided you meet the statutory requirements.
