Obtaining Russian citizenship

We help foreign nationals obtain Russian citizenship. We identify the right legal route, develop an immigration strategy tailored to your circumstances and guide you through each stage of the application.

What does Russian citizenship offer?

Russian citizenship establishes a lasting legal relationship with the Russian Federation. You no longer need to extend your visa, confirm residence under a permanent residence permit (VNZh) or maintain your immigration status as a foreign national.

Live in Russia without immigration restrictions
Obtain a Russian passport
Work without restrictions associated with foreign citizenship
Return to Russia freely
Receive the protection of the Russian Federation
Exercise the rights of Russian citizens, subject to statutory requirements

Russian citizenship also entails the duties of a Russian citizen, which you should consider when planning your move.

Main routes to Russian citizenship

Standard procedure

As a general rule, an adult foreign national with legal capacity may apply for citizenship after five years of permanent residence in Russia, counted from the decision granting a permanent residence permit. Applicants must demonstrate knowledge of Russian language, history and law unless exempt, and satisfy the other statutory requirements.

Simplified procedure

Certain categories of foreign nationals may obtain citizenship without waiting five years and, in some cases, without demonstrating knowledge of language, history and law or first obtaining a temporary residence permit (RVP) and permanent residence permit (VNZh).

The specific requirements depend on the legal grounds for applying.

Main routes to citizenship under the simplified procedure

Citizenship through parents or children

For holders of a permanent residence permit who have at least one parent or a son or daughter who is a Russian citizen residing in Russia.

Citizenship through marriage and a child together

For foreign nationals permanently residing in Russia who are married to a Russian citizen residing in Russia and have a child together, including an adopted child. Marriage alone does not qualify under this route.

Citizenship based on ancestry

For holders of a permanent residence permit with direct ancestors born or permanently resident in the Russian Soviet Federative Socialist Republic (RSFSR), or in territories of the Russian Empire or USSR within Russia's present-day borders.

Citizenship for former Soviet citizens

For former Soviet citizens permanently residing in Russia who were born or permanently resided in the RSFSR. Special routes also exist for stateless persons who previously held Soviet citizenship.

Citizenship for graduates with honours

For permanent residence permit holders who obtained an honours degree in Russia through a full-time, state-accredited higher education programme.

Citizenship based on education and employment

For permanent residents of Russia who completed a qualifying Russian education programme and worked in Russia in their corresponding field for a total of at least one year before applying.

Citizenship for nationals of certain countries

Special procedures apply to nationals of Belarus, Kazakhstan and Moldova after obtaining permanent residence. Requirements vary according to nationality.

Citizenship for children

We assist with establishing citizenship by birth or applying for citizenship for a child where one parent is or becomes a Russian citizen, and in other circumstances provided for by law.

Legal basis: Article 15 and Article 16 of Federal Law No. 138-FZ of 28 April 2023, On Citizenship of the Russian Federation; special rules apply to certain categories.

From Decree 702 to Russian citizenship

The Shared Values programme allows you to begin your move by obtaining a temporary residence permit without a quota or exam. Decree 702 itself is not a legal basis for citizenship. You may subsequently obtain permanent residence and apply for citizenship if you meet the statutory requirements.

MOVING TO RUSSIA

State programme for the resettlement of compatriots

The programme is intended for compatriots who wish to move to Russia permanently. Family members covered by the programme may also participate.

Programme participants and their family members residing in Russia may apply for citizenship under a special procedure, including without first obtaining temporary or permanent residence where the applicable requirements are met. Under the standard participation route, the conditions of the chosen region must be considered.

We assist with participation in the state programme: we assess eligibility, help you choose a region, prepare your documents and plan your future citizenship application.

RETURNING TO YOUR ROOTS

Repatriation to Russia

Since 2024, a separate repatriation procedure has operated within the state resettlement programme. It may be suitable for people with historical or family ties to Russia, including those born and raised abroad.

Who may qualify for repatriation?

  • People who previously formally renounced Russian citizenship voluntarily.
  • Former Soviet citizens born or permanently resident in the RSFSR or in the territory of the former USSR within Russia's present-day borders.
  • People whose direct ancestors were born or permanently resident in the RSFSR or in territories of the Russian Empire or USSR within Russia's present-day borders and held the corresponding citizenship.
  • Russian citizens permanently resident abroad as of 24 February 2022, for the purpose of returning to Russia rather than acquiring citizenship again.

What are the advantages of repatriation?

  • Choose any Russian region, including Moscow and St Petersburg, regardless of whether it participates in a regional resettlement programme.
  • Simplified documentation requirements for participation in the state programme, including no need to demonstrate Russian language proficiency at this stage.
  • Move together with family members covered by the programme.

Participation in the repatriation programme and subsequent acquisition of citizenship are separate procedures with different requirements. Foreign nationals granted participant status as repatriates may apply for permanent residence without an RVP (Article 8(2)(21) of Federal Law No. 115-FZ). Special conditions apply when submitting an application to join the programme from within Russia. State support for repatriates is more limited than under the general resettlement procedure; available measures are determined by the programme. Russian ancestry alone does not automatically confer citizenship.

Frequently asked questions

How many years must I live in Russia to obtain citizenship?

Generally, five years from the decision granting permanent residence. Exceptions apply to certain categories of foreign nationals.

Can I apply for citizenship immediately after obtaining permanent residence?

Yes, if you qualify for a special route that exempts you from the five-year residence requirement. Examples include qualifying family circumstances, Russian higher education with honours or documented ancestry.

Can I obtain Russian citizenship without temporary or permanent residence?

In certain cases, yes. For example, participants in the state compatriot resettlement programme and their family members may apply under a special procedure after moving to Russia if they satisfy the applicable requirements.

Do I need to pass a Russian language exam?

Under the standard procedure, you must demonstrate proficiency in Russian and knowledge of Russian history and the fundamentals of Russian law. Certain categories, including a number of applicants under Article 16 of Federal Law No. 138-FZ, are exempt.

Do I need to renounce my existing citizenship?

As a general rule, Russian law does not require applicants to renounce another citizenship when acquiring Russian citizenship. However, you should check the law of your country of citizenship and any applicable obligations.

Can I obtain citizenship under Decree 702?

Decree 702 provides a route to temporary residence, not citizenship. You may subsequently obtain permanent residence and apply for citizenship under a standard or special route if you meet the relevant requirements.
