WE ARE PARENTS TOO

We understand what it means to move with children

Moving abroad with your family raises far more questions than simply obtaining a visa or residence permit. Where will your children go to school? How do you find a good nursery? Where is the best place to live? How can you help your children adapt to a new language, make friends and feel at home?

As parents ourselves, we understand these concerns. We want to help not only with your immigration documents but also with making sure your family can settle comfortably into its new surroundings.

We have helped large families move to Russia, including families with several children. We know how important it is to plan ahead for the needs of every family member, from the youngest children to the adults.

We help you do more than move to Russia. We help you start your life here.

How we can help your family

Visas, residence permits and citizenship

We develop an immigration strategy for each family member, help prepare the documents and coordinate the timing of their applications.

Housing and choosing where to live

We help you choose a region, city and neighbourhood based on your budget, parents' employment, schools, nurseries and local amenities. We provide support with renting or buying property.

Nurseries and schools

We help you find suitable state or private educational institutions, understand admission requirements and prepare the necessary documents.

Helping children settle in

We help you find Russian language lessons, child development centres, extracurricular activities and sports clubs, taking into account each child's age, interests and individual needs.

Healthcare

We help you understand health insurance, registering with a local clinic where eligible, and finding the specialists you need.

Everyday life in Russia

We help with residence registration, banking and the practical and administrative matters that arise after arrival.

EDUCATION AND SETTLING IN

Nurseries and schools in Russia

We recommend planning your children's education before moving. Your choice of school or nursery will often determine which neighbourhood is most convenient for your family.

Nurseries

We help you understand admission procedures, availability and the choice between state and private nurseries. Where necessary, we help find a nursery with a suitable schedule and arrangements for settling in.

Schools

We help identify options based on your child's age, previous education and level of Russian. We also help prepare documents and clarify enrolment procedures.

Special admission requirements apply to foreign children entering Russian schools. As a general rule, they must demonstrate that they are lawfully staying in Russia and pass a Russian language test. We therefore recommend starting language preparation in advance.

How we organise your family's move

  1. Before the move. We discuss your family's plans and needs, identify immigration routes, and consider your preferred region, budget, housing and children's education.
  2. Preparing documents. We prepare an individual checklist for each family member and review birth and marriage certificates, translations and legalisation requirements.
  3. Arriving in Russia. We help organise immigration formalities, accommodation and the most urgent practical matters.
  4. Life after the move. We help with enrolling children in educational institutions, settling in as a family and further residence applications.

Frequently asked questions

Can my whole family move to Russia under Decree 702?

Yes, provided each family member has an applicable legal basis for entering and residing in Russia. We assess eligibility for adults and children and recommend an appropriate order for their applications.

Does every family member need the same immigration status?

No. Depending on nationality, age, family relationships and other circumstances, spouses and children may qualify under different legal grounds.

Can my child attend a Russian school if they do not speak Russian yet?

As a general rule, foreign children must demonstrate that they are lawfully staying in Russia and pass a Russian language test for school admission. We recommend starting language preparation before moving.

Can you help us find a nursery or school?

Yes. We help you identify options, understand admission requirements, prepare documents and communicate with educational institutions.

Does the whole family need to arrive in Russia at the same time?

Not necessarily. It may be more practical for one adult to arrive first, arrange accommodation and prepare for the rest of the family's move. Check each family member's entry requirements and timing in advance.

Can we contact you if we have not decided where to live yet?

Yes. During a consultation, we can discuss different regions, immigration routes, your children's needs, housing and the steps involved.

Planning to move to Russia with your whole family?

Tell us about your family and your plans. We will help you work out where to start, which documents to prepare and how to organise your move around your circumstances.
