Permanent residence permit (VNZh) in Russia

What is a permanent residence permit?

A permanent residence permit (VNZh) is a document confirming a foreign national's right to live permanently in Russia, leave the country and return freely.

As a general rule, the permit has no fixed expiry date. Special validity periods apply to certain categories of foreign nationals and forms of the document.

With permanent residence, you can:

Live in Russia permanently
Work without a work patent or work permit
Choose where to live and work
Access compulsory health insurance (OMS)
Set up a business and invest
Enter Russia without a visa
Study in Russia
Apply for Russian citizenship later, if eligible

Unlike a temporary residence permit, permanent residence is generally not tied to a particular Russian region.

Main routes to permanent residence

Permanent residence after temporary residence

The standard route requires you to obtain a temporary residence permit (RVP) first.

Generally, you can apply for permanent residence after eight months of residence under an RVP, but no later than four months before it expires. Permanent residence can be granted after one year of residence under an RVP.

Main routes to permanent residence without an RVP

Family-based permanent residence

For foreign nationals with parents or children who are Russian citizens permanently residing in Russia. Additional requirements depend on the family circumstances.

Permanent residence for graduates with honours

For foreign nationals who have graduated with honours from a Russian university after completing a full-time, accredited higher education programme.

Permanent residence for skilled professionals

For foreign nationals employed in Russia in occupations on a designated list, subject to work experience and employment requirements.

Permanent residence for highly qualified specialists (HQS)

For highly qualified specialists and their family members. The permit may be tied to the validity of the work permit. An indefinite residence permit is possible if additional requirements are met.

Permanent residence for IT specialists

For foreign specialists working under a contract with an accredited Russian IT company and their family members, subject to statutory requirements.

Permanent residence for investors

For foreign nationals who have invested in Russia in accordance with criteria established by the Russian government, and their family members.

Permanent residence based on ancestry

For foreign nationals with direct ancestors born or permanently resident in the Russian Soviet Federative Socialist Republic (RSFSR), or in territories of the Russian Empire or USSR within Russia's present-day borders.

Permanent residence based on nationality

Special procedures apply to nationals of Belarus, Kazakhstan, Moldova and Ukraine.

Permanent residence after education

For foreign students who have completed a qualifying programme after obtaining a temporary residence permit for educational purposes.

Other routes to permanent residence

Russian law provides additional special routes. We can help you determine which ones apply to your circumstances.

Legal basis: Article 8 of Federal Law No. 115-FZ of 25 July 2002, On the Legal Status of Foreign Citizens in the Russian Federation.

From Decree 702 to permanent residence in Russia

The Shared Values programme allows eligible foreign nationals to obtain a temporary residence permit without a quota or examination.

You may subsequently apply for permanent residence if you meet the applicable legal requirements.

We recommend planning for permanent residence from the outset, even before moving to Russia.

What to do after obtaining permanent residence

An indefinite permanent residence permit does not exempt you from immigration obligations.

  • Register at your place of residence in accordance with the applicable procedure.
  • Submit an annual notification confirming your residence in Russia.
  • Meet the applicable income and employment requirements.
  • Do not spend more than six months in total outside Russia during a calendar year, subject to statutory exceptions.
  • Replace your residence document on time when required by law.

Frequently asked questions

How long is a Russian permanent residence permit valid?

Generally, it has no fixed expiry date. Special validity periods apply to certain categories of foreign nationals and forms of the document.

Can I obtain permanent residence without temporary residence?

Yes. Russian law provides several routes to permanent residence without first obtaining a temporary residence permit.

Can I obtain permanent residence through marriage to a Russian citizen?

Marriage alone does not provide a direct route to permanent residence. However, your family circumstances may qualify you for temporary or permanent residence under other legal grounds.

Do I need to take a Russian language exam?

Generally, you must demonstrate knowledge of Russian language, Russian history and the fundamentals of Russian law. Exceptions apply to certain categories of foreign nationals.

Can I work in any region of Russia with permanent residence?

Yes. As a general rule, permanent residents are not restricted to a particular region when choosing where to work.

Can I obtain Russian citizenship after permanent residence?

Yes. Generally, you can apply after five years of permanent residence, counted from the decision granting your permanent residence permit, if you meet the other statutory requirements. Special routes are available to certain categories.
