Immigration disputes: representation before courts and government authorities

We protect the rights and legitimate interests of foreign nationals in Russia. We represent you before government authorities and courts at all levels, challenging unlawful decisions, actions and failures to act.

MORE THAN 20 YEARS OF LEGAL PRACTICE

We do more than prepare documents. We protect your rights.

Immigration problems can affect everything that matters to you: your family, work, business, education and ability to continue living in Russia.

SalutRussie is a team of lawyers led by Lev Vtiurin, in legal practice since 2005. Alongside immigration applications, we handle complex legal disputes, including disputes with government authorities.

For every case, we develop a tailored legal strategy, assess the evidence and prospects, and represent you at the necessary stages.

If your rights have been violated, we can help you protect them.

Cases we can help with

Refusals of temporary residence, permanent residence or citizenship

We challenge unlawful refusals of temporary residence permits (RVP), permanent residence permits (VNZh) or citizenship, examine the reasons for decisions and help restore your rights.

Refusal to accept documents and failure to act by immigration authorities

We protect your interests when Russia's Ministry of Internal Affairs (MVD) imposes unjustified requirements, misses application deadlines or refuses to accept documents.

Cancellation of residence permits and visas

We examine the reasons for cancellation, develop a strategy to retain or restore lawful status, and challenge decisions where legal grounds exist.

Entry bans to Russia

We help identify the grounds for an entry ban and the authority that imposed it, and challenge the decision through administrative or judicial proceedings.

Administrative expulsion

We defend clients in administrative offence proceedings and appeals against decisions, taking into account family ties, length of residence and other legally relevant circumstances.

Deportation and decisions declaring your stay undesirable

We assess the legality of deportation decisions and decisions declaring your stay or residence in Russia undesirable. We prepare documents and represent you in appeals.

Register of Controlled Persons

We help establish why you were entered in the register, assess your immigration status and identify lawful ways to resolve violations, including challenging erroneous inclusion.

Immigration fines and other disputes

We assist foreign nationals and employers facing administrative penalties or disputes concerning migration registration, permitted stay and employment.

Where we represent you

Russia's Ministry of Internal Affairs (MVD)

Local migration departments, regional offices and the central administration. We prepare applications, motions and complaints, and assist with dealings with officials.

Russia's Federal Security Service (FSB) and other government authorities

We represent clients in matters within the FSB's jurisdiction, including border authorities, and liaise with prosecutors and other competent authorities under the applicable procedures.

Courts of first instance

We prepare administrative claims, complaints, objections, motions and evidence, and represent you at court hearings.

Appeals, cassation proceedings and the Supreme Court of Russia

We challenge judicial decisions in higher courts, including the Supreme Court of the Russian Federation where the applicable procedure allows. We can join a case at any stage.

We handle cases in Moscow, the Moscow region and other parts of Russia. We agree the form of participation and representation based on the circumstances of your case.

TIME MATTERS

Received a refusal, summons or expulsion decision?

Appeal deadlines in immigration disputes can be very short. The sooner we receive your documents, the more options we may have to protect your rights in time.

If a territorial MVD authority refuses or cancels an RVP or VNZh, the deadline to appeal to the federal MVD is three working days, or three months to apply to a court, from receipt of the notification (Article 7 and Article 9 of Federal Law No. 115-FZ, in Russian). Other decisions may have different deadlines.

For an initial assessment, please provide:

  • A copy of the decision, order, notice or other documents you have.
  • The date you received the decision or the date of your scheduled hearing.
  • Your nationality, immigration status and the circumstances of your case.

You can contact us even if you do not have all the documents yet. We will help identify what you need to obtain and what steps to take.

How we work

  1. Case assessment. We examine documents, circumstances, the legal grounds for decisions and applicable deadlines.
  2. Legal strategy. We identify the best approach: submissions to government authorities, administrative appeals, court proceedings or a combination of these routes.
  3. Putting the strategy into action. We prepare procedural documents and evidence, and represent you before authorities and courts.
  4. Further support. Where needed, we appeal judicial decisions and assist with enforcement and restoration of your rights.

Before we start, we discuss the prospects of your case, the scope of our services and the fee. You understand the steps ahead and why they are needed.

Frequently asked questions

Can I appeal a refusal of a temporary or permanent residence permit?

Yes. We examine the reasons for refusal and your documents, identify the appropriate appeal procedure and assess the prospects of your case.

What if the MVD refuses to accept my documents?

Record the circumstances of your application and keep all available documents and correspondence. We can help identify how to protect your rights and prepare the necessary submissions.

Can an entry ban to Russia be lifted?

In cases provided for by law, an entry ban can be challenged. You need to identify the authority that imposed it, the grounds for the ban and circumstances that may affect the outcome.

Do you represent clients in appeals and cassation proceedings?

Yes. We handle cases before courts of first instance, appeal and cassation, and, where procedurally available, the Supreme Court of the Russian Federation.

Can I contact you from outside Russia?

Yes. The first consultation and document review can take place remotely. If necessary, we will agree arrangements for a power of attorney and representation.

Can I remain in Russia while appealing a decision?

It depends on the type of decision, your status and the applicable appeal procedure. Filing an appeal or claim does not always automatically preserve your right to stay. Where legally justified, we can ask the court for interim protective measures (Article 85 of the Code of Administrative Judicial Procedure, in Russian). We assess your right to stay and the availability of such measures individually.

Need professional legal representation?

Tell us about your situation. We will assess the circumstances, identify available legal remedies and propose a plan of action.
