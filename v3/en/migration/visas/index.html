Visas for Russia

Visas we can help you with

Private visa

For visiting relatives, loved ones and friends in Russia, including long-term private visas for close relatives of Russian citizens.

Decree 702 visa

A special private visa for participants in the Shared Values programme who plan to move to Russia.

Learn more about Decree 702 →

Tourist visa

For travelling, exploring Russia and visiting as a tourist.

Business visa

For business meetings, negotiations, conferences and other legally permitted business purposes.

Work visa

For foreign nationals planning to work in Russia. Support for both foreign employees and employers.

Student visa

For foreign nationals enrolling at Russian educational institutions.

Electronic visa (e-visa)

A simplified visa application procedure for citizens of eligible countries travelling for purposes permitted by law.

Other visas

Humanitarian visas, visas for temporary residents and other special visa categories.

How we can help

  • Choose the appropriate visa for your travel plans.
  • Prepare and review documents.
  • Help arrange an invitation to Russia if required.
  • Assist with dealings with Russian consulates and the Ministry of Internal Affairs (MVD).
  • Help with visa extensions where legally permitted.
  • Provide legal assistance with visa refusals, overstays and other complex situations.

Your visa is part of your immigration strategy

If you plan to obtain a temporary residence permit (RVP) or a permanent residence permit (VNZh), we recommend planning the entire process in advance rather than simply obtaining an entry visa.

Choosing the wrong visa can complicate your future plans.

Did you know about the Russian e-visa?

Citizens of eligible countries can apply for a Russian electronic visa themselves through the official website of the Russian Ministry of Foreign Affairs.

The visa is valid for 120 days and allows a stay of up to 30 days from entry, within the visa's validity period. It permits a single entry.

Already in Russia?

We also provide legal assistance to foreign nationals who are already in Russia:

  • Your visa is about to expire.
  • You want to change the legal basis for your stay in Russia.
  • You have applied for temporary or permanent residence.
  • You are having difficulties extending your visa.
  • You have breached immigration rules or received a refusal.

We will help you identify possible solutions.
