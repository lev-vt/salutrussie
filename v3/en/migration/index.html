What should you prepare for your first consultation?+

Start by describing your situation and plans in your own words. If possible, prepare:

  • Citizenship and country of permanent residence of each applicant.
  • Your objectives, preferences and intended dates of entry into Russia.
  • Copies of existing documents (you can send them in advance or we can request what is needed separately).
  • Information about your family, work, studies or business that may affect your plans.
