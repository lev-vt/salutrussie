The Shared Values programme offers one of the most accessible ways to move to Russia for foreign nationals who share traditional Russian values. If you qualify, you can obtain a three-year temporary residence permit (RVP) without a quota or an exam in Russian language, history and law. You can also apply for a special three-month private visa to enter Russia.

Find out if you qualify

The programme is open to foreign nationals who share traditional Russian values and wish to move to Russia from their country of citizenship or permanent residence. Government Order No. 2560-r sets out the eligible countries and territories. When applying, you will need to explain your reasons for moving under Decree 702.

Foreign nationals permanently residing in one of the listed countries may also participate, even if they hold a different citizenship.

For a preliminary assessment, we need your citizenship, country of permanent residence, previous visits to Russia and family circumstances. Citizenship of a listed country does not automatically entitle you to residence status: you still need to prepare the required documents and follow the immigration procedures. In our experience, however, this is the most predictable route to a temporary residence permit.

If you are already lawfully in Russia, you can also use the programme, including while holding a work or tourist visa. The application procedure depends on your documents and region of stay.

If you live outside your country of citizenship

If you are a citizen of a listed country but live in another country, you may also participate in the programme. When applying for a private visa in a third country, you may need to prove your right to long-term lawful residence; some consulates require more than six months. Agree your plan with an immigration lawyer and prepare the documents from your country of citizenship in advance.

Our approach: plan your move and its timeline first. Then apply for your visa and book your tickets. This helps you work towards obtaining your residence permit without unnecessary delays.

01
Before leaving your country

Obtaining a visa and preparing documents

Decree 702 provides for a three-month, single-entry ordinary private visa. Prepare the documents correctly, indicate your intended travel dates and request the appropriate period. The Russian consulate determines the visa’s validity dates.

Most importantly, obtain your criminal record certificate before travelling. For an RVP application in Russia, the certificate is valid for three months from the date of issue. The document must be apostilled or otherwise legalised if required. You can arrange a notarised Russian translation after arriving in Russia. From 1 February 2027, the requirements for criminal record certificates change. As a general rule, you will need paper certificates from your country of citizenship and your country of permanent residence, issued no more than three months before applying. Statutory exceptions apply.

Pay particular attention to electronic certificates and e-apostilles. If your country issues the certificate only electronically, discuss its preparation with an immigration lawyer in advance. Verification of these documents can vary across Russian regions. It is best to resolve any issues before travelling.

Prepare your passport, proof of residence, statement of reasons for moving and visa application form; check the rules of the consulate where you intend to apply.

You will also need medical insurance. We can arrange insurance through our official partner, Soglasie insurance company.

Important at this stage
  • Specify the correct travel dates and requested visa period. Errors in applications prepared independently sometimes result in a shorter visa.
  • Calculate the criminal record certificate's validity correctly: it must still be valid when you submit your application to Russia’s Ministry of Internal Affairs (MVD).
  • If you are already lawfully in Russia, the RVP application procedure may depend on the region of stay and visa category.
  • The special private visa allows one entry. If you need to leave while the application is being considered, you may need a new visa to return.

Practical advice: first prepare an action plan and calendar, taking into account the criminal record certificate's issue date, the consular appointment and submission to the MVD.

Applying for temporary residence through a consulate

You can apply for RVP through a Russian consulate in your country of residence. The law permits this, although it is not often used. Most of the applications we have assisted with under this route have been through the Russian consulate in France.

The consulate receives your documents and forwards them to the Russian Ministry of Internal Affairs (MVD), which decides on your application. Therefore, choose your intended region of permanent residence in advance.

You will undergo medical examination, fingerprinting and photography after entering Russia.

Applying through a consulate may extend the total process by approximately two months. But it can sometimes be more convenient: you finish your affairs abroad while the MVD considers the application and arrive closer to the expected decision instead of waiting in Russia.

This route has practical complexities. We recommend working with an experienced immigration lawyer.

02
The first days after crossing the border

Arrival in Russia: what to do first

Welcome to Russia!

Once you arrive, complete the immigration formalities promptly to keep your residence application on track.

  • Obtain your migration card on entry and keep it safe. Do not lose it.
  • Complete migration registration at your place of stay (often simply called registration). At a hotel, the administration submits the notification no later than the following working day; in a rented apartment, the general period is seven working days, and your host normally submits it. It is important not only to submit the notification but also to obtain confirmation that the MVD has accepted it: the acknowledgement slip or an electronic confirmation.
  • Prepare notarised translations of foreign documents.
  • Choose the region where you genuinely intend to live and submit your application.
  • If necessary, obtain a social insurance number (SNILS), set up access to the Gosuslugi government services portal, mobile phone service and banking: these are useful practical steps, not universal conditions for obtaining RVP.
What not to do after arrival
  • Do not pay for registration at an address where you do not actually stay. Registration must correspond to your actual place of stay; fictitious registration can entail serious liability, including expulsion and an entry ban.
  • Do not choose a region merely because someone claims that applications are processed faster there. Your temporary residence permit is tied to a specific Russian region. You can travel throughout Russia, but you must choose the region where you intend to live.
03
Preparing to apply to the MVD

Medical documents, translations and application

At this stage, we prepare the RVP application, check foreign documents and translations, complete the medical examination, obtain necessary identification numbers and carry out other procedures.

The medical examination for RVP must be completed in Russia, at an authorised medical facility. If you applied through a Russian consulate, you must complete the medical examination within 30 calendar days of entering Russia. Where documented valid reasons prevent you from meeting this deadline, you must complete it within 30 calendar days after those circumstances cease.

In the chosen region, find the medical centre's address and hours and undergo mandatory fingerprinting and photography. At the same time, we help arrange your social insurance number (SNILS), tax identification number (INN) and other documents for life in Russia.

Temporary residence application checklist
  • Passport and notarised Russian translation.
  • Current criminal record certificate, legalisation (for example, apostille) and notarised translation in Russia.
  • Application and questionnaire explaining the reasons for moving under Decree 702.
  • Medical documents in the prescribed form.
  • Two photographs measuring 35 × 45 mm and the state fee.
  • Family information — included in the questionnaire.

To demonstrate lawful stay, keep your visa (where required), migration card and proof of migration registration at hand.

This checklist is a starting point, not an exhaustive list of legal requirements. Before submitting, check the list against current legislation and MVD regulations or with an immigration lawyer. Documents from certain countries may have particular features, such as different spellings of names, legalisation and translation requirements.

We check that documents meet the requirements before applying to the MVD: expired certificates, inaccurate translations and application errors often cost applicants time and can reduce the likelihood of a positive decision.

04
Personal submission and awaiting the decision

Submitting your temporary residence application

Submit the RVP application to the regional office of the Ministry of Internal Affairs (MVD) in the region you have chosen for residence. Submission requires a complete set of documents and compliance with immigration rules. We assist applicants and liaise with officials.

Federal legislation is uniform throughout Russia. However, our experience shows that its application can vary by region.

Proof that the Ministry of Internal Affairs has accepted your application is one of the most important documents to obtain. Article 5(2) of Law No. 115-FZ allows you to lawfully await the decision in Russia after your original visa expires if the MVD has accepted the RVP application and required documents. This continuation of lawful stay must be properly documented and supporting evidence obtained. Documentation procedures may vary between regions and visa categories.

Under the standard procedure for applicants entering on a visa, the statutory decision period is up to four months. In practice, the entire process from preparing and submitting documents to receiving a decision may take two to six months. It is therefore better to plan your relocation calendar in advance.

Particularly important at this stage
  • Check reception hours, appointment procedures and document requirements at the competent office.
  • Do not use inaccurate information or fictitious registration.
  • Keep your migration card, proof of acceptance of the RVP application, proof of registration at your place of stay and medical insurance with you.
  • Monitor migration registration at your actual place of stay separately and extend it in time when required.
  • If you plan to travel outside Russia while the application is being considered, arrange the visa required for your return in advance.
✓
The next stage of life in Russia

After obtaining your permit: what changes

Once your temporary residence permit is issued, you have legal temporary resident status in Russia. The permit normally lasts three years. You become eligible for compulsory medical insurance (OMS), and you can work, study, conduct business, buy property and invest.

This allows you to plan your life, work and business in Russia within the conditions of your status, but you must also comply with the obligations attached to your status.

What should you know after receiving RVP?
  • Register at your place of residence according to the prescribed procedure. Never use fictitious migration registration.
  • You can work in the Russian region where your RVP was issued, subject to statutory exceptions.
  • You can travel throughout Russia while maintaining your residence and registration in the issuing region. Changing your region of residence requires a special procedure.
  • Submit annual notification of residence in Russia and legally required documents to the MVD. From 1 January 2027, RVP under Decree 702 is excepted from the new income-related cancellation ground. The annual notification requirement remains.
  • From 1 February 2027, holders of a temporary residence permit under Decree 702 will be able to apply for a new permit on the same grounds, no earlier than six months and no later than four months before their current permit expires. This is a new application, not an automatic extension.
  • To retain RVP, do not spend more than six months in total outside Russia in a calendar year, subject to statutory exceptions.
  • After eight months of residence under your temporary residence permit, you can apply for a permanent residence permit (VNZh), provided you meet the legal requirements, including an examination where mandatory. VNZh is generally issued without a fixed expiry date.
  • Under the general citizenship route, you can apply after five years of permanent residence from the date of the decision to grant VNZh. Other grounds are available for certain categories.

Frequently asked questions about moving under Decree 702

Do I need to know Russian?

No. RVP under Decree 702 does not require a document confirming knowledge of Russian language, history and legislation. This allows you to start learning Russian after moving and prepare for VNZh later.

Can I move with my whole family?

Yes, of course. We have already helped families of different sizes from Europe and the United States move to Russia. We prepare the route and documents for each family member in advance.

Can I apply for a temporary residence permit if I am already in Russia?

Yes. It is important to know which region you are in and what visa you entered on. Some regions have particular document acceptance procedures and requirements for criminal record certificates, including their form and verification of authenticity.

Is the special Decree 702 visa mandatory?

No, it is not mandatory. This visa is convenient for the standard route, but you can apply for RVP under Decree 702 while lawfully in Russia on another visa, including an electronic, tourist, business or work visa. Regional practice varies, so discuss the application procedure with an immigration lawyer in advance if you hold another visa.

What if my visa expires before my residence permit is issued?

Article 5(2) of Law No. 115-FZ allows you to await the MVD decision in Russia if the RVP application and necessary documents have already been accepted. Further lawful stay must be documented with the MVD under the applicable procedure. Do not leave this until the final day of your visa.

When can I obtain permanent residence and citizenship?

Under the general rule, you can apply for VNZh no earlier than after eight months of residence under RVP, if you have confirmed knowledge of Russian language, Russian history and the fundamentals of Russian legislation (for example, by passing an examination). Men aged 65 and over and women aged 60 and over are exempt from demonstrating such knowledge; other statutory exceptions apply. If approved, you will receive a permanent residence permit without a fixed expiry date. Under the general citizenship procedure, you can apply after five years of permanent residence from the date of the VNZh decision. Other grounds and programmes may be simpler in your individual case.

How long does it take to obtain a temporary residence permit under Decree 702?

The law provides a consideration period of up to four months for foreign nationals who entered on a visa. The overall duration depends on preparing documents, obtaining a visa and the region of submission.

Can I work in Russia after receiving my temporary residence permit?

Yes. After obtaining RVP, you can work without a work patent or work permit in the region where it was issued, subject to statutory exceptions.
