DECREE 702 · SHARED VALUES

Move to Russia under Decree 702Shared Values · a special residence route

The Shared Values programme allows qualifying foreign nationals and stateless persons to apply for a three-year temporary residence permit (RVP) without a quota or proof of knowledge of Russian language, history and legislation. A special single-entry private visa may be issued for entry. Each applicant must meet the conditions and general requirements.

Eligibility

Check whether the programme fits your situation

Presidential Decree No. 702 of 19 August 2024 creates a route for people wishing to move from their country of citizenship or permanent residence for the reasons specified in the Decree. Government Order No. 2560-r of 17 September 2024 identifies the countries and territories. Citizenship alone is not enough; the motive, residence circumstances and general grounds for refusal must also be assessed.

We review citizenship, country of permanent residence, earlier visits to Russia, family members and intended region. If you are already lawfully in Russia, an application may be possible without a special visa.

Entry rules for particular citizenships, including Ukraine, differ. Verify the applicable list and consular jurisdiction before travel.

Living outside your country of citizenship?

The Decree also refers to permanent residence in a listed country. A consulate in a third country may require proof of lawful long-term residence. Check its rules before booking travel.

Our approach: plan the route and calendar first, then apply for the visa and book travel.

1
Before travelling

Visa and document preparation

Decree 702 provides for a single-entry ordinary private visa for three months, subject to the decision of the Russian consular authority. Official consular practice may describe it as up to 90 days or up to three months; always check the dates printed on the actual visa.

Prepare the criminal-record certificate before travel. For a visa applicant submitting for RVP, its three-month validity from issue is important. Legalize it by apostille or another applicable procedure, unless an applicable treaty dispenses with legalization. Arrange a certified Russian translation.

Electronic certificates and e-apostilles deserve particular attention: the method of verifying authenticity can create practical difficulties. Confirm the approach in your intended region.

Check the passport, written motives, visa application, insurance and the competent consulate’s current requirements.

Important before travelling
  • Plan the certificate's issue date, visa dates and filing appointment together.
  • Check entry permission for each family member.
  • The special visa is single-entry. Do not assume you can return on the same visa after leaving Russia.

Applying through a Russian consulate

The law also permits an RVP application through a Russian diplomatic or consular institution in the country of residence. The consulate receives the documents and forwards them to the MVD for its decision. Choose your intended Russian region first. Medical examination, fingerprinting and photography are completed in Russia according to the applicable procedure. The route may take longer, but may be convenient if you need to finish commitments abroad.

2
After crossing the border

Arriving in Russia

Welcome to Russia. Complete the formalities promptly after arrival.

  • Keep your migration card if issued.
  • Arrange migration registration at the actual place of stay. Hotels generally notify by the following working day; the general rule for other accommodation is seven working days, subject to exceptions.
  • Keep the accepted notification or electronic confirmation.
  • Arrange notarized translations and plan the region where you actually intend to reside.
  • SNILS, tax number, banking, mobile service and public-service access may be useful but are not universal RVP prerequisites.

Never buy a fictitious registration. Your address must reflect actual residence. A convenient filing location is not a substitute for a genuine choice of region.

3
Preparing your file

Medical examination, translations and application

Prepare the application, check documents and translations, and undergo medical examination in an authorized medical organization in Russia. Under Article 6(4.1) of Law No. 115-FZ, a consular RVP applicant must undergo the examination within 30 calendar days of entering Russia.

Confirm the applicable fingerprinting, photography and filing procedure with the competent office.

Document checklist
  • Passport and notarized Russian translation.
  • Current criminal-record certificate, legalization where necessary and certified translation.
  • Application and questionnaire stating the motives under Decree 702.
  • Medical documents in the required form.
  • Required photographs and state fee, where applicable.
  • Evidence of lawful stay and family information.

The exact list depends on the current regulations and your circumstances.

Check the documents before visiting the MVD. Expired certificates, inconsistent translations and mistakes in the application can delay the procedure.

4
Filing and waiting

Submitting an RVP application

Submit to the competent territorial MVD office in the region where you intend to reside. Federal law is uniform; local administrative practice and appointment arrangements may differ.

Obtain evidence of formal acceptance of the application and required documents. Article 5(2) of Law No. 115-FZ provides an exception to the duty to leave at expiry of the previous stay if the relevant residence application is duly accepted. Document the continuation of lawful stay and verify visa and registration arrangements separately.

The ordinary visa-based RVP application has a statutory consideration period of up to four months. Preparation, appointments and issuance are additional stages. Do not rely on a universal total timeline.

Important while waiting
  • Keep your passport, visa where applicable, migration card, registration confirmation and proof of acceptance.
  • Maintain lawful migration registration at your actual place of stay.
  • Check re-entry documentation before travelling abroad.
  • Filing does not by itself create a right to work.
5
Living in Russia

After receiving RVP

A temporary resident can generally work without a work permit or patent in the issuing region, subject to statutory rules, and may obtain compulsory medical insurance if eligible. Business, education and property arrangements are governed by their own legal requirements.

Key obligations after approval
  • Register at the actual place of residence as required.
  • Observe the regional residence and employment rules, including the procedure for moving regions.
  • File annual residence notifications and documents required by the applicable law.
  • Under the rules entering force on 1 January 2027, RVP granted on the Shared Values ground is exempt from the new income-related cancellation ground; annual notification remains required.
  • Avoid absence from Russia exceeding six months in total in a calendar year, subject to statutory exceptions.
  • As a general rule, an application for permanent residence may be made after eight months of RVP residence, no later than four months before RVP expiry; check the language and other requirements.
  • General citizenship eligibility ordinarily requires five years of permanent residence from the VNZh decision; special grounds may differ.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need Russian language proficiency?

For an RVP under Decree 702, no document confirming knowledge of Russian language, history or law is required. Later VNZh requirements must be checked separately.

Can my family move with me?

Yes, if each family member has a lawful route and the necessary documents. A permit is not automatically transferred to relatives.

Can I apply while already in Russia?

Potentially, if you are lawfully staying and meet the programme conditions. Check your current visa and the filing region.

Is the special visa mandatory?

No, not in every situation. Compare your existing lawful entry and status with the applicable procedure.

What if my visa expires while my application is pending?

Article 5(2) of Law No. 115-FZ may allow you to remain after formal acceptance. Obtain the applicable evidence and separately check visa and registration formalities.

When can I apply for permanent residence?

Under the general RVP route, after at least eight months of residence, subject to the filing deadline and requirements. Direct VNZh grounds may exist.

How long does RVP take?

For the ordinary visa-based procedure, consideration is up to four months; preparation, appointment and issuance add time.

Can I work after receiving RVP?

Generally without a patent or work permit in the issuing region, subject to legal exceptions and employment rules.
