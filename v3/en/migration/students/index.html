SPECIAL IMMIGRATION STATUS

What is a temporary residence permit for education?

A temporary residence permit for educational purposes (RVPO) allows an international student to live temporarily in Russia for the duration of their studies plus 180 calendar days after the study period ends.

Who can obtain a permit for education?

Foreign nationals studying full-time at a state educational institution or state research organisation on one of the following programmes:

  • Bachelor's, specialist degree and master's programmes.
  • Medical residency and assistantship-internship programmes.
  • Postgraduate research programmes, including military postgraduate programmes.

Programmes subject to state accreditation must be accredited. Language courses or preparatory programmes alone do not qualify for this permit.

Benefits of a residence permit for education

No temporary residence quota
Duration of studies + 180 days
A route to permanent residence after graduation

Apply to the Ministry of Internal Affairs (MVD) in the region where you study. Before transferring, changing your mode of study or leaving your programme, check the implications for your residence permit.

Working while studying

International students enrolled full-time on qualifying programmes at educational or research institutions may work in their free time without a work patent or work permit, subject to Article 13 of Federal Law No. 115-FZ.

The right to work and the right to remain in Russia after graduation are separate matters. If you plan to work after graduating, identify a legal basis for your continued stay in advance.

How can you stay in Russia after graduation?

Permanent residence after an education-related permit

After completing a qualifying programme at a state educational or research institution, RVPO holders can apply for permanent residence. The law allows applications within three years of graduation without first obtaining an ordinary RVP. This period does not itself extend your right to stay in Russia.

Permanent residence for graduates with honours

An international graduate who has completed a full-time, accredited higher education programme in Russia and obtained an honours degree can apply for permanent residence without first obtaining temporary residence. Learn more about permanent residence for honours graduates →

Working after graduation

We help identify a legal basis for working and living in Russia after graduation, taking into account nationality, field of study, employer and current immigration status.

Russian citizenship

Special citizenship routes are available to permanently residing foreign nationals with a Russian honours degree, or with qualifying Russian professional education and one year of work in their corresponding field. Learn more about Russian citizenship →

Legal basis: Article 8 of Federal Law No. 115-FZ; Article 16 of Federal Law No. 138-FZ.

Problems with immigration documents?

We assist international students and graduates in complex situations, including when:

  • Your student visa is expiring while an application for a new status is pending.
  • Your university has not extended your permitted stay or there are problems with migration registration.
  • You are transferring to another university, taking academic leave or ending your studies.
  • The MVD refuses to accept or grant your application for an education-related or permanent residence permit.
  • You are at risk of losing your lawful immigration status.

We assess your documents, identify lawful options, prepare submissions to universities and the MVD, and protect your rights where necessary.

Frequently asked questions

Can I obtain an education-related residence permit if I am already studying in Russia?

Yes, if you study full-time at a state educational or research institution on a qualifying programme and meet the other requirements.

Can I obtain an education-related residence permit at a private university?

Studying at a private university alone does not qualify you for an RVPO. However, you may have other legal routes to living and working in Russia.

Do I need an honours degree to obtain permanent residence after an RVPO?

No. An honours degree is not a mandatory requirement for the special route after completing studies with an RVPO. Permanent residence for honours graduates is a separate route.

Can I work on a student visa?

Yes, subject to the statutory requirements for full-time study and work during your free time. You should check your programme and the validity of your immigration status.

What if my student visa expires while my permanent residence application is pending?

Check in advance whether you have a legal basis to extend your visa and permitted stay in your particular circumstances. Do not assume that submitting an application automatically extends your stay.

Can I stay in Russia after graduating from university?

Yes, if you have a legal basis, for example through an education-related permit followed by permanent residence, an honours degree, employment or family circumstances. It is best to choose your route before graduating.
