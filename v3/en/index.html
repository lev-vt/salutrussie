SPECIAL PROGRAMME FOR SHARED VALUES

Move to Russia under Decree 702Shared Values Programme

A special immigration procedure for foreign nationals who meet the requirements of Russian Presidential Decree No. 702.

We help you assess eligibility, develop an immigration strategy and prepare documents for a special private visa valid for up to three months, followed by an application for a temporary residence permit (RVP).

Start by checking whether your country of citizenship is included in the current programme list. Visa and residence permit decisions are made by the competent authorities.
