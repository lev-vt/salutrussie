Payment for legal services

Despite difficulties with international bank transfers, we work with clients worldwide and help identify an available payment method.

Common payment methods

  • Russian bank card — if you already have a card issued by a Russian bank.
  • Visa or Mastercard issued by a foreign bank — through a specialist payment service, subject to availability for your card.
  • Other international payment methods — on request, taking into account the country, amount and applicable requirements.

We agree the payment method and terms individually on request. Payment is made after the fee has been agreed and you have received payment details or a payment link.
