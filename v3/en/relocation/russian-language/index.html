RELOCATION AND SETTLING IN RUSSIA

Learning Russian

Speaking Russian can help you feel more confident in everyday life, communicate with others, work and study in Russia. We help foreign nationals and their families find a teacher and a suitable way to learn.

Discuss your learning needs →

Why do you want to learn Russian?

Russian for everyday life

Communicating in shops, on public transport, at healthcare facilities and government offices. Practical language skills for independent life in Russia.

Russian for work and business

Communicating with colleagues, clients and business partners, developing professional vocabulary and business communication skills.

Russian for students and children

Help finding learning options for students and children who need to adjust to a Russian-speaking educational environment.

Exam preparation

We help determine whether you need to demonstrate Russian language proficiency for immigration purposes and find suitable preparation for the relevant examination.

Find a learning format that suits you

We consider your current level, native language, goals, relocation plans and time available for lessons.

  • One-to-one lessons with a teacher.
  • Group lessons or language courses.
  • Online lessons before and after moving.
  • Separate programmes for adults and children.

We help you find specialist teachers and educational providers. The programme, schedule and lesson fees are agreed separately with the provider.

Do I need to take a Russian language exam?

Requirements for demonstrating Russian language proficiency depend on your immigration route and individual circumstances. Certain categories of foreign nationals are exempt.

We help establish which requirements apply and, if necessary, find suitable exam preparation. Even when an examination is not required, learning Russian makes settling in and everyday life easier.
