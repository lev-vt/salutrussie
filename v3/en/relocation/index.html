Relocation and settling in Russia

We help foreign nationals and their families prepare for their move, organise the first steps after arrival and settle into life in a new country. We provide support with legal, administrative and everyday practical matters.

How we can help

Administrative procedures

We help you navigate paperwork, migration registration, tax identification numbers (INN), social insurance numbers (SNILS), the Gosuslugi government services portal and dealings with public authorities, taking your immigration status into account.

Moving is about more than paperwork

Obtaining a visa, temporary residence permit (RVP) or permanent residence permit (VNZh) is an important part of moving. But once you arrive, you also need to find a home, deal with administrative procedures, arrange your children's education and adjust to your new surroundings.

As parents ourselves, we understand the importance of considering the needs of the whole family. We help develop a relocation and settling-in plan tailored to adults and children alike.

Support before and after arrival

  • Before the move. We help identify the right immigration route, prepare documents, plan accommodation and work out the first steps.
  • After arrival. We help you navigate the necessary administrative procedures, deadlines and everyday practical matters.
  • As you settle in. We support you with new challenges relating to residence, family, employment and education.

We can assist with individual matters or agree a comprehensive plan. Where necessary, we coordinate with specialist partners and advisers.
