RELOCATION AND SETTLING IN RUSSIA

Practical support for everyday life in Russia

In a new country, even familiar everyday tasks can be challenging. We help foreign nationals and their families settle into life in Russia, understand local services and make the arrangements they need.

Discuss your needs →

How we can help

Housing and setting up your home

We help find accommodation with our partners, communicate with landlords, negotiate rental terms and arrange essential household services. Help with renting a home →

Banking services

We help you understand the requirements for opening bank accounts, obtaining cards and using banking services, taking your status into account.

Mobile phone services and internet

We help identify the documents and procedures needed to obtain a SIM card, arrange home internet and use digital services.

Healthcare

We help you understand health insurance requirements, access to compulsory health insurance (OMS) and voluntary health insurance (DMS), choosing healthcare providers and obtaining medical care.

Nurseries and schools

We help you choose educational institutions, understand admission requirements and identify the necessary documents. Moving with your family →

Transport and everyday services

We help you navigate driving licence requirements, buying and registering a car, public transport and local services.

Taking the whole family's needs into account

We understand that moving with children requires particular preparation. It is important to plan not only housing and paperwork, but also education, healthcare, familiar routines and the everyday needs of each family member.

We help develop an individual plan for the first steps after arrival, establish priorities and address practical matters as they arise.

Practical support in Russia

We help you understand your particular situation, explain the necessary steps and documents, and coordinate with partners, interpreters and specialist advisers where required.

We can assist with a single practical matter or agree comprehensive support as you settle in. We establish the scope of assistance, region and arrangements for involving other specialists in advance.
