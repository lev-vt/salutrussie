RELOCATION AND SETTLING IN RUSSIA

Administrative procedures

We help foreign nationals navigate Russian administrative procedures, obtain the necessary documents and deal with government authorities. We explain what needs to be done, where and in what order.

Discuss your needs →

How we can help

Migration and residence registration

We help identify the applicable procedure for registration at your place of stay or place of residence, prepare documents and communicate with your landlord.

Tax and social insurance numbers (INN and SNILS)

We assist with obtaining a tax identification number (INN) and a social insurance number (SNILS), and checking your details in the relevant government systems.

Gosuslugi and online government services

We help you navigate account creation and verification, access public services, submit online applications and obtain the necessary confirmations.

Notifications and mandatory procedures

We help establish which obligations apply to you, including annual notifications for temporary residence permit (RVP) and permanent residence permit (VNZh) holders, medical examinations and fingerprint registration where required.

Documents for family members

We help you navigate documentation for spouses and children, requirements for foreign certificates, translations and document authentication.

Dealing with government authorities

We prepare applications and submissions, and help arrange visits to the Ministry of Internal Affairs (MVD), multifunctional public service centres (MFCs), tax authorities and other agencies. Where necessary, we accompany you in person or represent you where permitted by law.

Every foreign national's circumstances are different

Not every procedure is mandatory for every foreign national. Required documents, deadlines and procedures depend on nationality, immigration status, region of residence and individual circumstances.

We identify the steps you actually need to take and develop a clear plan tailored to your situation.

From consultation to in-person support

We can assist with a single administrative procedure or coordinate several applications as part of one settling-in plan.

  • Explain the procedure and prepare a checklist of required documents.
  • Help prepare applications and arrange appointments.
  • Accompany you to government offices in the agreed region where necessary.
  • Help address refusals, errors in documents and other difficulties.
