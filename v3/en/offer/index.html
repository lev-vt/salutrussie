Public offer for paid legal consultation services

Terms for agreeing, providing, paying for and terminating consultation services.

Draft for approval. Not yet in force. Payments are not taken directly on this website.

1. Provider, subject matter and scope of the offer

Innovative Legal Solutions LLC, hereinafter the Provider, operating under the SalutRussie brand, offers to enter into a paid legal consultation services agreement on the terms set out below. The Client is an individual with legal capacity, including a sole trader, or an organisation acting through an authorised representative.

The service consists of legal analysis of the situation described by the Client and advice in the agreed format: orally, by video call, in writing or by another means. Drafting separate documents, representation, translation, notarial and other services are not included unless expressly agreed in a separate order. Ongoing legal support and B2B projects are governed by separate agreements.

2. Formation of the agreement

Before acceptance, the Provider sends the Client an individual proposal (order) specifying the subject and scope of the service, format, duration or delivery period, consultant details where relevant, the price in roubles and, if needed, an agreed equivalent in another currency, the payment terms and deadline, and the applicable version of this offer. The order and these terms together constitute an offer to enter into an agreement. An enquiry through the website does not constitute an agreement before an agreed order is issued.

Acceptance means full and unconditional agreement to the terms of the specific order, expressed through the agreed payment within the specified period or another method expressly stated in the order. The agreement is concluded when the Provider receives acceptance in accordance with Russian law. An initial enquiry alone does not create an obligation to pay. The parties retain electronic correspondence and order confirmations; documents and messages may serve as evidence of the formation and performance of the agreement.

3. Fees and payments

The price, including applicable taxes, payment deadlines and procedures are communicated before the agreement is concluded. Additional services and expenses are agreed in advance and are not automatically included in the fee. Payment is made using the Provider's payment details or an agreed payment service. The availability of payment methods, settlement currency and potential fees are agreed before acceptance, taking applicable requirements into account. Additional paid services are provided only with the Client's prior consent. A fiscal receipt is issued in the cases and manner prescribed by Federal Law No. 54-FZ. Neither party is required to provide the other with bank card details, verification codes or passwords.

4. Provision of services

  • The Client provides the necessary accurate information and documents through an agreed secure channel, and informs the Provider of deadlines and previous decisions.
  • The Provider identifies the legal issues, analyses the relevant law and documents, and delivers the service within the agreed period and in the agreed format.
  • Any rescheduling of the consultation is agreed between the parties. If additional work becomes necessary, the Provider agrees its scope, timing and price in advance.
  • An oral consultation is considered delivered when the agreed meeting has taken place; a written consultation is considered delivered when the agreed output has been sent through the specified channel. This does not restrict the right to make claims concerning deficiencies or delays.

5. Quality, confidentiality and liability

The Provider delivers services in good faith and professionally, based on information supplied by the Client and the law in force at the time of the consultation. Legal advice does not guarantee the issuance of a visa, temporary residence permit (RVP), permanent residence permit (VNZh), work permit, court decision or any other outcome dependent on third parties.

The Provider maintains confidentiality of information received within the requirements of law and the agreed terms. Disclosure to third parties is permitted where there is a lawful basis. The parties are liable in accordance with the law; these terms do not exclude or restrict the Client's mandatory statutory rights, including consumer rights.

6. Withdrawal and refunds

The Client may withdraw from the agreement at any time before the services have been completed by giving notice by email or another agreed communication channel. If the Client withdraws for reasons unrelated to a breach by the Provider, settlement is made in accordance with Article 782 of the Russian Civil Code and, for consumers, Article 32 of the Russian Consumer Protection Law: taking into account services actually delivered and documented expenses incurred for the specific order, to the extent permitted by law and without double counting. Any unused advance payment is refunded. No withdrawal penalty or unconditional non-refundable payment is provided for.

The Provider may withdraw in the cases and with the consequences prescribed by law, including compensation for the Client's losses where Article 782(2) of the Russian Civil Code applies. Refunds are made within the statutory period or, where no specific period is prescribed, without undue delay and through an agreed lawful procedure. Claims concerning deficiencies and delays are handled in accordance with applicable law.

7. Personal data

Personal data is processed to conclude and perform the agreement, comply with legal obligations and in other cases where there is an appropriate legal basis. The procedure is described in the personal data processing policy. Where consent is necessary, it is obtained separately from other documents and information confirmed or signed by the data subject (Article 9 of Federal Law No. 152-FZ).

8. Complaints and dispute resolution

The Client may submit a complaint to salutrussie@salutrussie.ru or by another means permitted by law. The parties seek to resolve disagreements through negotiation. Russian law applies, subject to mandatory provisions applicable in the particular case. Consumers retain their statutory choice of court and other protections. Mandatory pre-litigation procedures apply only where required by law.

9. Version of the terms

The version communicated to the Client when agreeing the order applies to the concluded agreement. Changes to published terms do not amend an existing agreement without a lawful basis. Draft version dated 24 November 2026.
