Terms of paid legal consultations

Terms for booking, delivery, payment and cancellation.

Review draft; not yet in force. Online payments are disabled in this preview.

1. Provider and scope

The legal service provider is ООО «ИННОВАЦИОННЫЕ ПРАВОВЫЕ РЕШЕНИЯ» (the Provider), trading under SalutRussie. These terms cover paid legal consultations for individuals and authorized representatives of organizations. A consultation consists of legal analysis and advice on the agreed question, orally, by video or in writing. Representation, document drafting, translations and extended casework are not included unless separately agreed. Long-term and B2B work is governed by a separate agreement.

2. Booking and acceptance

Before payment, the Provider sends an individual order identifying the question, scope, format, duration or delivery deadline, total price, currency, payment terms and applicable version of these terms. The individual order and these terms form the offer. Acceptance occurs through the agreed payment or another expressly agreed method. An initial inquiry is not a binding order. Electronic correspondence and order confirmations may serve as evidence.

3. Price and payment

The total price, including applicable taxes, is disclosed before acceptance. Additional work and expenses require prior agreement. Payment is made to the Provider or through its confirmed payment provider. Fiscal receipts are issued where required under Federal Law No. 54-FZ. Never share card credentials, one-time passwords or banking passwords.

4. Performance and cooperation

The client supplies accurate information, relevant deadlines and necessary documents through an agreed secure channel. The Provider performs the agreed consultation professionally and within the agreed time. Changes to scope, time and price require agreement. An oral consultation is delivered when the session has taken place; a written consultation when the agreed result is sent. This does not restrict statutory remedies for defective or delayed performance.

5. Quality and confidentiality

Advice is based on the information supplied and applicable law at the time of the consultation. The Provider does not promise an immigration decision, visa, work permit, court outcome or decision of another authority. Confidential information is handled according to law and the agreed terms. Mandatory statutory rights and liability are not excluded.

6. Cancellation and refunds

The client may cancel before completion by emailing the Provider. Charges for work actually delivered and documented expenses incurred for the particular order are calculated only to the extent permitted by law, without double counting. Any unused advance is refunded. There is no automatic forfeiture or cancellation penalty. The Provider may terminate only subject to applicable legal requirements, including compensation under Article 782(2) of the Russian Civil Code where applicable. Refunds and remedies follow mandatory statutory deadlines, or are processed without undue delay if no specific deadline applies.

7. Personal data

Personal data are processed on appropriate legal grounds to arrange and perform the contract and meet legal duties. See the privacy policy. Any consent required by law is obtained separately from acceptance of the contract.

8. Complaints and governing law

Send complaints to salutrussie@salutrussie.ru. We seek an amicable resolution. Russian law applies subject to mandatory applicable consumer and conflict-of-laws provisions. Statutory venue options and consumer remedies remain available. A pre-action complaint is mandatory only where the law requires it.

9. Version

The version communicated with the agreed order applies to that contract. Later changes do not alter existing contracts without a lawful basis. Draft dated 24 November 2026. The Russian draft is the reference text; mandatory rights are unaffected.
