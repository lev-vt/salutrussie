# SalutRussie localisation standard

Approved 2026-11-24.

- Use natural, professional English and French, not literal translation. The approved RU content is the legal reference; do not introduce new legal grounds or guarantees without verification.
- English: temporary residence permit (RVP), permanent residence permit (VNZh). French: permis de séjour temporaire (RVP), titre de séjour permanent (VNZh). Russian source: разрешение на временное проживание (РВП), вид на жительство (ВНЖ).
- Exact Latin transliterations: RVP and VNZh, case-sensitive. No TRP, PRP, VNZH or other substitutes. Introduce the full localised term before using the abbreviation on each standalone page; prefer plain-language titles and menu labels. Preserve official Russian abbreviations in Russian source text.
- Temporary residence is not temporary stay; a permit is a document or authorisation, not merely the residence status. Education-related residence must be explained separately.
- Preserve all substantive legal qualifications, links, personal attribution, accessible navigation and language-specific prefilled contact messages. Use British/international English consistently.
- Work in thematic batches, checkpoint before changes, check links, mobile layout, SEO/GEO and legal consistency before marking each batch complete.
